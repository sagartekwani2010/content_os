# Recording kit

One page to follow at every shoot. It exists because the 14 November 2026 recording (one 47-minute take,
read from a clipboard, in a swivel chair) could not be cut cleanly. Background:
`feedback/learning-mission-final-edit-diagnosis-2026-11-15.md`.

## Phone

- HDR video off. 4K at 30 fps, so punch-ins stay sharp.
- Lock exposure and focus on your face (long-press, then lock).
- Phone on a tripod at eye level, lens clean, portrait.

## You

- Fixed stool or standing on a marked spot. No swivel chair.
- Mic clipped at the same place for the whole shoot; do not touch it between takes.
- Nothing in your hands unless the beat calls for it.

## Method: line by line

1. Load the beat's line into the teleprompter (Edits has one) placed at the lens, or learn the line.
2. Look at the lens, deliver the line, stop. Do it two or three times.
3. Stop recording. One clip per beat keeps takes easy to find; the last good take wins.
4. Between beats, change the frame on purpose when the shoot plan says so: closer, wider, new angle.

If the reel is voiceover-led: record the voice as audio only, sitting still, one paragraph at a time.
Perform to camera only for the hook and the close.

## Every shoot

- Ten seconds of room silence.
- The B-roll list from `shoot_plan.md`, shot the same day: screen recordings, hands on the keyboard,
  over-the-shoulder, the messages or pages the script mentions.
- Screenshots of other people's messages are cropped to the message text before use: no names,
  handles or photos.

## Before you leave

- Play back one clip with sound. Check focus, level, and that your eyes are on the lens.
- Every beat in the shoot plan has at least one take you would be happy to post.

## Continuity between takes (from Ep 2, 18 Nov 2026)

Cuts between takes showed in Ep 2 (same chin-on-hand pose for the whole reel; hands and energy changing across
cuts). The teleprompter now shows, per beat, FEEL (what the viewer should feel), FACE/VOICE (delivery) and
POSE (start/end pose and energy).

- Base pose: sit back, hands resting together, shoulders square to the lens. Reset to it before every take.
- Hold 1 s on the lens before speaking and 1 s after the last word.
- Start each beat at the energy the previous beat ended on.
- Gestures mid-line; back in the base pose before the last word.
- One sitting, beats in order, same clothes, hair, glasses, light and chair.
