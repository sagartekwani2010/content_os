choose by complete wording, direct eye contact across the **whole phrase**, and
natural delivery. Source transcript timestamps may drift; verify the final
render's speech and mouth/eye position at each join. Preserve enough room for
phrases to finish. The v3 cut is a long review assembly, not a 30–40 second
posting cut, and no performance claim exists for it.
