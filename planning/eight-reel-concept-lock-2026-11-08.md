# Eight-Reel concept lock — 2023 → 2026 → next direction

**Date:** 8 November 2026 (Asia/Kolkata)<br>
**Decision:** The creator requested a final 6–8 Reel slate using three approved categories. This document locks **eight concepts** for the next two-week experiment: three mass-entry evolution explainers, two recent research tests, and three original agent demos. It does not constitute a production brief, evidence that any run succeeded, or approval to spend, schedule or publish.<br>
**Public promise:** “I show how AI moved from answering prompts to learning from outcomes, then test whether my own agents actually work.”

## Editorial contract

- Each evolution Reel uses **2023 baseline → 2026 evidence → future research direction**. “Future” is explicitly a hypothesis or open problem, not a forecast or announced feature.
- One viewer question and one mechanism per Reel. A repeated gym check-in gives the training concepts continuity; fitness remains a real test environment, not exercise advice or a claim that AI produced physical progress.
- A paper Reel must move from a precise author claim to a small independent test or a clearly stated test design, then show what the test cannot establish. The two selected papers were posted within about 30 days of this lock. Community attention is a selection signal, never evidence that a finding replicated.
- Each demo must show authentic input, the system's actual decision/action trace, the human boundary and observed outcome. No fabricated success, gain, inventory or model-weight change.
- Use the creator's approved learning memory for hooks, narration order, visual timing, export QA and the feedback loop after generation and finalization.

## Production gates and substitutes

1. Write a human-authored brief for each selected concept with the six immutable fields, then script and create keyframes. Nothing here asserts a result or authorizes media credit spend.
2. **Wardrobe pair:** actual owned inventory, one occasion, real rejection and saved traces. If either trace is missing, capture it before the paired scripts.
3. **RSI Arena:** do not present the paper's 1,000 GPU-hour setup or reported percentage as a local reproduction. Separate author-reported numbers from Sagar's test.
4. **CUA-Master:** use a contained computer-use fixture, not live consequential actions. Show the boundary between an inference-time retry and the paper's broader training loop.
5. **Astra–Qwen:** inspect local compute fit and collect reviewed examples and held-out cases before an improvement claim. If that cannot be completed in the window, slot 8 becomes the previously proposed Gym Assistant system-design trace (`REL-07`: missing data or pain → stop/ask/escalate); Astra remains next in the backlog. Do not replace a failed result with a success story.

## Two-week experiment and readout

- Publish four per week in slot order, subject to creator approval of production briefs and final exports. Pair the Wardrobe sequel within the same week as its first Reel when the calendar allows.
- Compare **organic follows per 1,000 non-follower accounts reached** after a full seven-day window for every Reel. Watch ratio, shares, saves, profile visits and AI-qualified comments explain the acquisition signal. Keep paid traffic and Trial Reel distribution separate.
- The last Reel's seven-day result arrives one week after the two-week publishing run. Eight Reels give directional evidence; replicate any apparent category winner before changing the standing mix.
- After each generation and final export, record the approved hook, actual media result, creator feedback and any measurable performance. Add only creator-approved or repeated learnings to versioned prompts and the brand learning memory.
