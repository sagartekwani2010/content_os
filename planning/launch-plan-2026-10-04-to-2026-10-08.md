## Per-Reel launch loop

### T−24 hours

- Story teaser that raises the Reel's unresolved question without revealing the
  answer.
- Wardrobe: poll `Would you let an agent pick from your own wardrobe?`
- SkillRL: poll `Does storing every past run mean an agent learned?`
- Career: poll `What worries you more: fewer openings or changing skills?`

### T−2 hours

- One source or proof preview: real wardrobe input, SkillRL paper title, or one
  labour-market source card.
- No countdown spam and no second feed post.

### Launch

- Cover communicates the learning promise in the central profile-grid safe
  zone.
- Caption adds one nuance instead of repeating the script.
- Reshare the Reel to Stories after publication with one specific reason to
  watch.
- Pin one creator comment containing the transferable rule or source boundary.

### First 60 minutes

- Reply to substantive comments with questions or additional evidence.
- Do not use identical one-word replies or engagement bait.
- Record repeated questions as candidates for follow-up content; do not change
  the strategy based on early view count.

### T+12 to 24 hours

- Share one comment, correction or missed nuance in Stories.
- Link the next Reel conceptually, not with a forced `part 2` promise.
- Capture reach, non-follower reach, average watch time, completion if
  available, saves, shares, profile visits and follows.
