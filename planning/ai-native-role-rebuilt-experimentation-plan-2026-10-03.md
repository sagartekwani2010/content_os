# Become AI-Native experimentation plan — Your Role, Rebuilt

## Status

`CREATOR_APPROVED_STRATEGY`

This plan defines how the function-specific career stream will be tested. It
does not authorize publishing or scheduling any individual Reel; each episode
still requires an approved brief and evidence review.

## Strategic decision

The durable stream is **Become AI-Native: Your Role, Rebuilt**.

Layoff anxiety is the top-of-funnel tension. The editorial product is a
function-specific mitigation playbook called **CRISP**:

1. **C — Compress commodity work** — use AI to accelerate routine work and reinvest the saved time.
2. **R — Reskill into adjacent capabilities** — learn the engineering, AI, domain and evaluation skills required by the new workflow.
3. **I — Integrate around an outcome end to end** — begin with the valuable decision and own its path across data, workflow, backend interfaces, deployment and action.
4. **S — Safeguard the system** — own evaluation, monitoring, failure boundaries and escalation.
5. **P — Prove outcomes** — create a portable artefact and measurable result that demonstrate ownership.

Every episode begins with the same broad layoff-anxiety claim or statistic and
then moves immediately into Sagar's personal or role-specific story. A
creator-supplied number may enter a draft, but it remains marked `NEEDS
VALIDATION` until approved for publishing.

CRISP reduces dependence on commoditised work and improves visible evidence; it
does not guarantee protection from layoffs. The stream never promises that a
career can be saved or future-proofed. Its
signature position is:

> You cannot future-proof a job title. You can build evidence that you know how
> to redesign the work and own the outcome.

## Experiment objective

Find which functional playbooks attract the most **qualified new followers**
without degrading save value, trust or fit with Sagar's AI-practitioner and
leadership authority.

The experiment is not a referendum on whether a profession matters. It tests
which audience/problem pair should receive the first deeper mini-season.

## Primary hypothesis

A role-specific Reel using the `CRISP` contract will
produce higher follows per reach than a generic AI-native career POV because a
viewer can immediately identify their own risk, action and required evidence.

## Why Data Science is the first stream

Start with **Data Science**.

- It is the strongest match for Sagar's practitioner authority, so the first
  followers acquired are more likely to be qualified and trust the teaching.
- The role has a crisp transformation to demonstrate: AI makes routine analysis
  and model-building cheaper while problem framing, evaluation, causal
  judgement, deployment decisions and business accountability become scarcer.
- It naturally bridges the career initiative to the dominant Agents, End to End
  initiative through agent-assisted analysis, evaluation and production
  workflows.
- A credible Data Science episode can earn saves and follows without forcing a
  broad fear claim merely to maximise raw reach.

This creator-selected starting point prioritises authority-qualified follower
growth. The controlled scan still tests whether another function converts more
followers per unit of reach.

## Functional scan order

| Order | Function | Acquisition hypothesis | Main risk |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Data Science | Strongest alignment with Sagar's authority; likely to attract qualified followers, saves and high-intent discussion. | Must go beyond generic notebook, SQL and AUC advice. |
| 2 | Product | Broadens from technical practitioners into decision-makers while preserving the leadership bridge. | Easily becomes generic unless one decision workflow is shown. |
| 3 | SDE | Largest technical audience; coding-agent anxiety can expand non-follower reach after authority is established. | Crowded creator market; generic coding claims will disappear. |
| 4 | Marketing | Tests reach beyond core technical roles through a workflow with obvious commoditisation pressure. | May attract followers too far from the agents and technical-career positioning. |

MLE is not a separate acquisition-scan episode in this first cycle. Treat it as
a specialised extension of the Data Science lane if the Data Science signal is
strong enough to justify a deeper mini-season.

## Phase 0 — umbrella launch

Publish one series-introduction candidate built around:

> Everywhere I go, people ask how to protect their careers from AI. The honest
> answer is that you cannot future-proof a title. You have to redesign the work.

It introduces the four-part contract and names the coming functional sequence.
It must not cite an unverified universal percentage of jobs or layoffs.

## Phase 1 — controlled four-function scan

Produce one Reel per function in the approved order: Data Science, Product, SDE
and Marketing. Hold these variables as stable as practical:

- face-led delivery plus no more than two proof visuals;
- 40–55 seconds;
- high-emotion but defensible role-specific hook;
- one real workflow or work product;
- the stable broad layoff opener followed immediately by the role-specific story;
- the `CRISP` story contract;
- sequel-style close, with no hard-sell CTA;
- comparable organic launch support and no paid spend during the first seven
  days unless every candidate receives the same treatment.

The intended changing variable is the **function/audience problem**. Do not
simultaneously change delivery mode, duration, CTA and visual density, or the
results will not be interpretable.

## Phase 1 episode contract

Each Reel must contain exactly one primary career decision:

1. an identity-level hook naming the obsolete or commoditised version of the
   role;
2. one verified source, demonstrated workflow or clearly labelled practitioner
   interpretation;
3. one concrete before/after workflow for that function;
4. one proof artefact the viewer can build or show;
5. one memorable contrast between shallow output and outcome ownership.

Do not combine SDE, Data Science, Product and Marketing recommendations in one
role-readiness matrix. That was useful for broad orientation but is too generic
for the recurring playbook.

## Measurement contract

Measure every candidate at the same checkpoints: 24 hours, 72 hours and seven
days. Use the seven-day result for selection.

### Primary metric

- **Follows per reach** — the role of this stream is follower acquisition, not
  raw views alone.

### Secondary diagnostics

- non-follower reach;
- profile visits per reach;
- shares per reach;
- saves per reach;
- average watch ratio or completion where available;
- comments or messages that identify a role and ask for the next playbook;
- production time and spend.

Interpret raw reach and follower conversion together:

- **High reach + high follow conversion:** scale the audience/problem pair.
- **High reach + low follow conversion:** the hook travelled but the teaching
  promise or profile positioning did not convert.
- **Low reach + high saves/conversion:** keep the role but test a stronger hook.
- **Low reach + low conversion:** deprioritise until stronger evidence or a
  better work example exists.

## Selection gate

Do not select a winning function until all four candidates have seven days of
organic data. Compare each result with the recent-account baseline, not a
generic industry benchmark.

Advance the top two functions when they satisfy both conditions:

1. follows per reach is at or above the 75th percentile of the most recent ten
   comparable organic Reels; and
2. either share rate or save rate is above the recent median.

If no role clears both gates, keep the strategy and revise the hook/proof
execution; do not conclude that functional playbooks are invalid after one pass.

## Phase 2 — three-Reel mini-seasons

The top two functions each receive three single-concept Reels:

1. **Threat map:** one work product AI is making cheap and why;
2. **Workflow rebuilt:** one before/after AI-native operating trace;
3. **Proof of ownership:** one portfolio, interview or workplace artefact that
   demonstrates judgement and outcome ownership.

Only inside a winning function should the next hook test compare an
identity-threat opening with an outcome-promise opening. Keep the content
contract and proof constant enough for the comparison to be useful.

## Evidence rules

- Company restructuring is evidence only when supported by an official
  announcement or filing.
- Distinguish a workforce reduction from proof that AI caused every eliminated
  role.
- Do not use “20–30% of overall cuts by 2027” unless a primary source supports
  that exact population, mechanism and horizon.
- Forecasts describe populations and scenarios; they do not guarantee an
  individual's safety or displacement.
- Clearly label Sagar's workflow and career recommendations as practitioner
  judgement when they are not measured labour-market findings.

## Reusable intellectual property

Preserve each role's task map, rebuilt workflow, proof rubric, interview prompt
and audience questions. Over time these become the foundation for career-proof
reviews, community sessions, workshops and coaching without turning the public
content into a hard sell.
