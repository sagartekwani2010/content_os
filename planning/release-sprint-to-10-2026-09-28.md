## Production operating rules

1. **Story before asset generation.** Lock the overall narrative, then its
   time-driven delivery, then create visual prompts.
2. **One claim per visual.** The visual must be actual source material,
   a disclosed estimate, an illustrated decision contract, or a research-backed
   simplification—never decorative AI imagery or a fake dashboard.
3. **Face carries the stakes.** For face-led Reels, begin and end on Sagar;
   visuals answer the question raised by the spoken line.
4. **No premature verdicts.** Log release, format, hook, duration, production
   time, paid spend, reach, follows and saves. Do not announce a format winner
   before the tenth release.
5. **Evidence disclosure stays visible.** Any simulation, estimate or external
   benchmark is labelled in the Reel and caption.

## What we will analyse after Reel 10

- Follows per reach and profile visits per reach
- Average watch time / completion, if available
- Saves and shares per reach
- Face-led-with-visuals versus talking-head-only comparison
- Hook type: personal observation, experiment result, paper claim, or practical
  consumer use case
- Production effort and money spent relative to follower and save quality

Do not interpret a single paid or organic post in isolation. The post-level
dashboard is a record; the weekend review is where we decide what to scale.
