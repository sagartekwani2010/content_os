# Demo vs Production experiment kit

Open `demo.html` in a browser at a 9:16 viewport and record three short clips:

1. **Happy path** — 2.5–3 seconds. Click nothing; hold on the green `PASS`.
2. **Tool fails** — 2.5–3 seconds. Click `Tool fails`; hold on the red `FAIL`.
3. **Guardrail** — 3–4 seconds. Click `Guardrail`; hold on the amber `GUARDED`.

Each clip is intentionally an illustrative simulation, not a claim about a
real food-delivery company or measured production result. Keep the footer in
the raw capture or add `Illustrative simulation` as a small overlay.

Use the clips as Slides 2, 4 and 6 in the carousel described in
`outputs/why-ai-demos-fail-experiment-carousel.md`.
