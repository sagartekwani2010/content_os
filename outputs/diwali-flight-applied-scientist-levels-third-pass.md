## Brief fingerprint

- Topic: Three Applied Scientist answers to a Diwali flight rebooking agent question
- Core thesis: All three Applied Scientist answers must deliver a confirmed ticket within the customer's constraints. Their judgment grows from selecting a viable option, to controlling a reliable transaction, to defining and evaluating the safe boundary of autonomy. The answers are illustrative scopes, not Amazon's official interview rubric.
- Learning objective: Viewers can distinguish the different judgments needed to select a viable option, execute a reliable booking, and set the agent's autonomy boundary.
- Format: Reel
- Brand pillar: Become AI-Native: Career & Leadership (`ai_career`)
- High-level sections: Diwali flight cancellation and ₹10,000 original fare with ₹5,000 extra-spend limit; all three answers produce a confirmed on-time ticket; L5 viable choice, approval and evaluation; L6 rebooking skill and transaction control; L7 autonomy boundary and failure evaluation; transferable distinction between option, transaction and autonomy judgment.

## Execution plan

**Learning payoff:** Distinguish judgment about the option, the transaction and the limits of agent autonomy. **Story:** salary ladder → cancelled Diwali flight → free rebooking arrives too late → all three candidates confirm a suitable on-time ticket → reveal the expanding decision scope. **Case assumption:** an on-time alternative exists within the customer's ₹5,000 additional-spend limit; its exact price is intentionally omitted. **Visuals:** one simple option contrast, one confirmed-ticket card repeated for each level, with one added decision layer each time. **Risk:** salary provenance and whether the spending limit means cash now or final net cost need verification or clarification.
