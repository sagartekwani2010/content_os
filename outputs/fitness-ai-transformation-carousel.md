## Brief fingerprint

- **Topic:** How I used AI to go from this to this
- **Core thesis:** AI did not train or transform me; it made the feedback loop
  between my goal, routine, data and adjustments easier to run consistently.
- **Learning objective:** Viewers understand a practical, non-magical way to use
  AI for planning and reflection in a fitness routine.
- **Format:** 8-slide carousel
- **Pillar:** AI x Real Life
- **Required evidence:** your before/after images, time period, one or two real
  AI interactions, and only metrics you personally tracked.

## Selected hook

> How I used AI to go from this to this.
>
> Not with a magic workout plan. With a better feedback loop.

## Caption

friction-heavy.

plan when a week did not go as expected.
