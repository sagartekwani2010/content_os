# Hybrid Gym Assistant — face-led Reel two

## Format decision

This is the **second Reel** in the sequence. Reel one can be picture-led; this
one is Sagar speaking, recorded in **16:9 landscape** and placed inside a 9:16
Instagram edit. Static insight cards appear above or briefly replace the frame
only when they prove the spoken point.

The story is not “look at my architecture.” It is: **Jev was much cheaper, so
why did I keep GPT? A real user message answers that question. Only after that
answer does the hybrid-versus-GPT-only comparison make sense.**

## Overall script — speakable, Instagram cut

Last Reel, Jev was 73% faster and 99% cheaper than GPT for one clean decision.
I still did not remove GPT.

This Gym Assistant message is why: “I slept badly and missed a workout. What
should I do?”

Jev cannot have that conversation. GPT can. GPT understands the message, asks
when something is missing, and turns it into a clean check-in.

Then I have a choice. GPT-only can make the next call too. It is simpler—and
for a one-off decision, it may be the best option.

I add Jev only when the next job repeats and is bounded: judge risk, then pick
progress, hold, ask for information, or human review. A system rule blocks
risky input from becoming automatic progress.

So Jev replaces one GPT decision step, not GPT itself. In this setup, that is
an estimated 36% less waiting and 49% lower model cost—not 73% and 99%. These
are estimates until I run live logs.

My rule for agents: use GPT for open-ended work. Split the system only when a
repeatable decision can prove it earns the extra complexity.

## Capture direction: 16:9 speaking footage in a 9:16 Reel

- Record at 4K if available, in horizontal 16:9. Keep head and shoulders in
  the central 50% of frame, with eyes slightly above centre; this protects the
  edit if the frame is resized.
- In the final 9:16 edit, keep the 16:9 speaking frame in the lower 35–40% of
  the screen from 9–46 seconds. Place the corresponding static insight card in
  the upper 55–60%. Use a warm off-white background around both so it feels
  intentional, not like a screen share.
- For 0–9s and 46–54s, make Sagar the dominant visual: use the 16:9 footage
  full-width with a clean paper-coloured frame rather than cropping aggressively.
- Cut to the static card exactly as the relevant noun is spoken: `message`,
  `check-in`, `route`, `blocked`, `GPT → Jev`, then `36% / 49%`.
- Speak the 99%, 49%, 36% and 73%/99% slowly. The 36%/49% card must keep
  `ESTIMATE · LIVE LOGS PENDING` visible for its entire on-screen duration.

## What to avoid while recording

- Do not begin by defining Jev or explaining the architecture. Begin with the
  apparently irrational choice to keep GPT despite Jev's component benchmark.
- Do not read the cost figures in dollars; the percentages are enough for the
  viewer. The exact numbers remain in the static result card and documentation.
- Do not say the hybrid is “better.” It is a modelled result until full logs
  exist, and the simple one-call GPT baseline still has to be tested.
- Do not end with a generic “follow for more.” The last sentence is the payoff.
