# Caption — AI Native, Part 1

Using AI is not the same as being AI-native.

Neither is refusing to use it. And neither is handing it the whole task.

After interviewing hundreds of candidates across engineering, product and
marketing, the gap I see most often is not tool knowledge. It is whether people
carry their own thinking into the tool.

The useful question is: did AI make your judgment better—or did it replace the
thinking you were meant to do?

My current definition:

1. Form a view first.
2. Carry the real context into the tool.
3. Use AI to challenge and expand your thinking.
4. Keep accountability for the decision.

Follow if you want to work AI-native—not just use AI. I’ll break down context,
evals, and the systems behind real agents.
