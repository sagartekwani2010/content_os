# AI Native — series introduction Reel

## Format

One stable, face-to-camera POV Reel. No B-roll, diagrams, screenshots, cutaway
assets or music-led transitions. Use only natural delivery and platform-native
captions. Record vertical 9:16; head-and-shoulders framing, direct eye contact,
clean background and even light.

## Overall script — recording-ready reference

Using ChatGPT or Claude does not make you AI-native.

And neither does giving AI the whole job and waiting for an answer.

I have interviewed hundreds of candidates across engineering, product and
marketing roles. The biggest gap I see is not tool knowledge. It is what people
do with their own thinking before they open the tool.

Recently, I spent ten minutes with a senior candidate brainstorming a problem:
the constraints, the options, the trade-offs. Then I asked them to use AI.
They opened a blank chat and started explaining the problem from scratch.

The context we had just created was gone.

They were using AI. But they were not using it as an extension of their
thinking.

To me, AI-native means three things. First, form a view before you open the
tool. Second, carry the real context into it—the constraints, the options you
already rejected, what success looks like. Third, use AI to challenge and
expand your thinking, not to hand it the steering wheel.

That is true whether you work in product, marketing, engineering or data. You
still own the decision. AI just makes your judgment bigger.

That is what AI-native means to me: do not hand your thinking to AI. Bring your
thinking to it.

Follow if you want to work AI-native—not just use AI. I will break down
context, evals, and the systems behind real agents.

## Recording notes

- Keep it as one take or use only invisible jump cuts between full sentences.
- Do not say the employer, candidate’s role level beyond `senior`, gender, or
  any detail that could identify the person.
- Do not use the words `agent`, `workflow`, `LLM`, or a tool comparison here.
  This Reel is about human judgment, which makes the series more accessible.
- Deliver `The context we had just created was gone` as the emotional centre of
  the Reel. It is the image viewers should remember.
- The platform’s auto-captions are enough; avoid designed text cards or a
  technical thumbnail inside the video.

## Story validation

| Test | Pass rationale |
| --- | --- |
| Question by 5 seconds | If tool use alone is not AI-native, what is? |
| Concrete proof | A short anonymised interview moment shows lost context. |
| Story turn | The blank chat reveals the difference between using a tool and carrying thinking forward. |
| Useful resolution | Think first, bring context, use AI to challenge, own the decision. |
| Earned continuation | Prompt versus context directly follows from the story; it is not a generic CTA. |
