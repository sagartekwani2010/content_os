# Brief: What makes an AI project credible to someone hiring: an eval, a failure you caught, and the fix, not certificates

- **core_thesis:** Anyone can demo an AI app. What separates an AI engineer is proving how it fails: build an eval, find the worst failure, fix it with a guardrail. The creator shows this with their own calorie tracker.
- **learning_objective:** Viewer can turn their own AI project into the four-line card: task, eval set, worst failure, fix.
- **format:** Talking head (bottom half) with hand-drawn line diagrams on top; stands alone, no recap of the Gym Jarvis series; reuse the Meal Analyser visuals (hidden-chicken plate, 499 vs 94, flagged plates).
- **brand_pillar:** AI careers from someone who does the work; credibility from building and testing AI on their own training and food
- **sections:** ['hook: the failure is worth more than a certificate', 'proof: the plate my AI got wrong (499 true vs 94 estimated; saw 35 g of 194 g chicken)', 'the method: eval on 50 weighed dishes, find the worst misses, add a confidence flag that caught 12 of the 15 worst', 'the save card: task, eval set, worst failure, fix', 'comment trigger + loop back to the hook']
- **audience:** Indian students and early-career engineers trying to get into AI roles, anxious about layoffs, collecting certificates and tutorial projects
- **promise:** In under a minute: the one thing that makes your AI project worth talking about in an interview.
- **takeaway:** Show how your AI fails and how you caught it: task, eval set, worst failure, fix.
- **target_duration_s:** 50
- **open_questions:** ["Is the creator comfortable claiming they interview or hire? If not, phrase as 'what I'd want to see' rather than hiring authority.", 'Comment trigger: is the creator willing to reply to project comments with an eval suggestion?']
- **facts_allowed:** {'true_kcal': 499, 'estimated_kcal': 94, 'chicken_true_g': 194, 'chicken_seen_g': 35, 'eval_dishes': 50, 'worst_misses': 15, 'worst_caught_by_flag': 12, 'protein_true_g': 45, 'protein_est_g': 12}
- **constraints:** ['Never invent hiring outcomes, salaries or job statistics', "No 'in my last video' recap; Gym Jarvis/Meal Analyser may be named only as 'my calorie tracker'", 'Numbers only from facts_allowed', '40-55 s', 'Creator records it seated, landscape, teleprompter']
