- **The emotional engine pays off twice.** "Months of weekends" opens the build in beat 2 and returns in beat 18 as the pride line with a laugh at his own expense: he built a coach that is honest about him.
- **The result arrives before the explanation.** The STOP frame is on screen in the first second and the 23-set tally by about 12 s, so mass viewers see the payoff before SEE / JUDGE / CHECK.
- **Three parts, one spoken idea each.** The voice says only "17 joints", "my best rep, not a textbook" and "both must agree"; the degrees, model name and rule detail stay on the diagram. The refusal to judge 4 sets and the lunge false alarm show the system admitting its limits, which is what finance and builder viewers trust.
- **Re-hooks sit at one third and two thirds.** "It refuses to judge" lands at about 18 s and "The one real STOP?" at about 37 s. The gym-buddy line is the forward, placed next to the FORM comment trigger.
- **The ending is sourced and loops.** It says "reportedly", shows the source tag and the real posting, and uses no numbers. "Come, let's find out" echoes "Come, let's see", and the last frame matches frame 1.
