## WHY THIS LANDS
- **Every part is someone the viewer already knows.** SEE is a friend filming you, JUDGE is a referee with a whistle, and CHECK is a second doctor's opinion. Model and tool names stay in small type, and each part plays on real gym footage with the node as a corner label.
- **The research line is back and it pays off.** The current best had dropped the locked beat 3b. Here the camera-angle warning lands at 10 s over a one-second lunge flash, and beat 16 closes it with "the warning came true".
- **Non-builders get two reasons to wait.** A mini-clip of the swung set stays pinned through the build-up, and a job tag is pinned from 17 s to the send beat. Re-hooks sit at about 24 s ("It refused to judge four sets") and 43 s ("The one real STOP?").
- **Both checker must-fixes are gone.** "I counted that set" is replaced by "Worst rep: under half my best" (53° against 122°), and "Here's why" by "Now, forget the gym." over "Gym Jarvis uses an AI model. This job trains one." No line claims a motive or anything unmeasured.
- **The gym story ends on a laugh and the ending is about the viewer.** His face reacts on the false alarm and the send beat, which holds for its full length. "Post-training" is glossed on screen, and the reel loops on "How do you get ready? Come, let's find out."

The script is 182 spoken words, estimated at about 60.7 s for both MAIN and TRIAL after 1.15x, with beats 1 and 16 held at 1.0x.
