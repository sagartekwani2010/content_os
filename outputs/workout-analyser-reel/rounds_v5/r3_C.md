## WHY THIS LANDS
- **Every part is someone the viewer already knows.** SEE is a friend filming you, JUDGE is a referee, and CHECK is a second opinion from another doctor. The courtroom is reduced to the creator's own "23 sets on trial", so "wrongful conviction" and "retrial" become "wrong call" and "check footage".
- **The hook is readable in frame 1 and paid off twice.** Frame 1 names REVERSE CRUNCH and tags the ghosts BEST REP 122° and WORST REP 53°. The reason arrives at 26 s inside JUDGE ("worst rep, under half my best"), and the confirmation at 44 s ("Both agreed"). The hook stays verbatim, and the cost is stated in measured numbers with no injury wording.
- **The architecture keeps its order but always shows a result.** Each part plays over his own footage with one phrase on screen and ends on a result: 4 sets refused, the reverse-crunch STOP, the lunge wrong call. The pinned "why STOP? ↓" thumbnail gives mass viewers a reason to wait, and the lunge is amber from its first frame so red belongs only to the real STOP.
- **Both checker must-fixes are resolved.** "I counted that set. It didn't count." is replaced by lines built only on FACTS. The bridge is now "Now, the part about your job.", which claims no motive for building Gym Jarvis.
- **The ending is aimed at every viewer's career.** The bridge line turns the Salesforce beat towards the viewer, post-training is glossed in plain words on screen, and FORM gets its own readable moment on the last beat, away from the send line at 46 s.
