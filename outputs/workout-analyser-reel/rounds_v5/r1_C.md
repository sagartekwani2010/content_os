## WHY THIS LANDS
- **Everyday pictures carry the architecture.** SEE is a friend filming you, JUDGE is a referee with one whistle, and CHECK is a second doctor's opinion. A viewer who doesn't code follows all three parts, while builders get the detail on the diagram (17 joints, Claude Fable 5.2, the both-must-agree rule).
- **The result is on screen in the first 4 seconds, and the loop closes by name.** The red STOP opens the reel, "23 sets on trial, it refused 4" re-hooks at one third, the lunge false alarm is the twist at two thirds, and "That one set? My reverse crunch" pays off the hook before the CTA.
- **The failure makes the system trustworthy.** "I had only turned to the camera" is a mistake any gym-goer can picture, and it explains why CHECK exists without a word of jargon.
- **One forwardable line.** "Send this to the buddy who swings every rep" is a gym-buddy joke that also states what the AI actually measured (short, swung reps), with no injury or risk claim.
- **The ending raises the stake for every cohort.** It moves from my set to your job: Salesforce reportedly letting people go while hiring for LLM post-training, over the real posting with the source tag. "Come, let's find out" echoes "Come, let's see".

The timings assume 2.8 words/s sped up 1.15x, with beats 1 and 19 at 1.0x: MAIN is 180 spoken words and runs about 61 s, TRIAL about 60 s.
