- **Each part is someone the viewer already knows.** SEE is a friend filming you, JUDGE is a referee and CHECK is a second opinion, with "like a second doctor" on screen. The joint count and frame rate are on-screen only, and the spoken time in SEE goes to the refusal ("It refused four sets.").
- **The hook's cost is readable in frame 1 without touching the spoken hook.** The big text is "WORST REP: UNDER HALF MY BEST" with the label "how far my legs lowered", and 122° and 53° are small tags. The date tag now reads "old phone footage, 15 Aug 2026 · analysed Nov 2026", so it no longer clashes with "Sept → Nov 2026".
- **The early dead stretch is shorter and carries a result.** The countdown is 1 s, and the 23-set wall fills in behind "months of weekends". A percussive bed keeps the pulse after the blast, so the three parts don't sound like a lecture.
- **The job ending is planted early and the payoff doesn't read as an end card.** A small "AT THE END: what this means for your job" tag sits from beat 6 and grows into "NOW: YOUR JOB" during beat 19, where the bed does not swell. The send prompt is on-screen only, FORM sits on the coach beats (15-16), and the last frame has one CTA, FOLLOW.
- **Both checker must-fixes are gone, and two new lines need the creator's sign-off.** Beat 18 uses only measured facts and the bridge is a question, not a motive. The lunge STOP is labelled "referee alone, before the both-agree rule", so "only one STOP" holds. The new humour lines are "Your gym buddy?
