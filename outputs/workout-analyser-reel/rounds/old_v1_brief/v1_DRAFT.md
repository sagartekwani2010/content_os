**Type:** hand-lettered marker caps for labels and diagram nodes; a monospace for every measured number, so numbers always look like readouts. Two weights at most.

**Pacing:** one visual change per beat, two in the hero beats. Motion eases in and out with no snap zooms; the fastest move in the reel is the 0.8 s lock-on in beat 1. Hold each finished drawing for at least 0.7 s before the next cut.
