- **The hook loop is closed by name.** Beat 1 opens "the one set I should never have done" and beat 19 repeats the phrase over the held STOP stamp. The middle becomes a wait for that answer, and the last frame cuts cleanly back to beat 1.
- **The reversal is staged as wrong, then right.** The AI calls STOP on the lunge (12), is wrong in silence at 1.0x (13), and "So who checks my AI?" (14) raises the doubt. The coach and the both-agree rule answer it (15-16), so "But on this set, both agreed" (17) lands as an earned verdict.
- **Contrast replaces explanation in the first 7 seconds.** "Green: my best rep. Red: my worst. Same set." puts 122° vs 53° on screen as one comparison, so a stranger sees the payoff before the launch sequence.
- **There is one comedic beat and one forwardable line.** "My crime? Turning to face the camera." gives the failure story a laugh. "Send this to that gym buddy" sits on the raw swing footage, where the fault is most visible.
- **The stake is a time fact.** "Three months on my phone" comes from the dates (filmed 15 Aug, found in Nov) and makes no injury claim. That line is my addition, so confirm it is true of the clip; the [RISK] words stay a slot for the creator.
