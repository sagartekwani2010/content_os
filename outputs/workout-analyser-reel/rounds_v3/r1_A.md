## WHY THIS LANDS
- **One question runs the whole reel.** Beat 1 shows the STOP and stamps "BUT IS IT REAL?" under it; beat 6 deepens the doubt with "It got one wrong". The answer only arrives at beat 16, "Both agreed", and beat 17 closes the hook phrase by name before the CTA.
- **Two promises are on screen before step one.** "IT GOT ONE WRONG" (amber, on the wall) is paid off at beats 12-13, and the corner tag "AT THE END → THE JOB SALESFORCE IS HIRING FOR" is paid off at beats 19-21.
- **Each step hands off to the next.** The 1/5-5/5 marker ends on a question each time: "BUT WHAT'S A REP?", "AND IF A JOINT HIDES?", "SO WHO JUDGES?", "THEN IT SLIPPED", "AND ON MY SET?". Steps 1-3 take 8 s in total, with the questions on screen so the voice stays short.
- **The stake is physical and carries no numbers.** "My legs stopped halfway. Momentum did the rest." says what a short, swung rep is; 122° vs 53° stays on screen. This replaces the spoken "Green: my best rep. Red: my worst." line, and "Three months on my phone" and the [RISK] slot are gone.
- **Two things to confirm before the shoot.**
  - **Bridge line:** I wrote "That's a job now" instead of "That's the job", because the posting describes a model-training role and FACTS does not say it covers a judge-and-coach check. Use "That's the job" only if the saved JD mentions evaluation.
  - **Runtime:** both cuts end at about 61.6 s, near the top of the 55-62 s window. If the takes run long, trim beat 11 to "Four: a judge compares each rep with my best." and leave "NOT A TEXTBOOK" on screen.
