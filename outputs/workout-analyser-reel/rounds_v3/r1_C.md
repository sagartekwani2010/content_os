## WHY THIS LANDS
- **The stake is physical and spoken in gym words.** "My legs stopped halfway. Momentum did the rest." and "Reps counted. Work not done." say what a short, swung rep costs; the 122° and 53° stay on screen. "Halfway" is a rounding of the measured range (most reps 60-80° against 122°), so confirm you are happy saying it.
- **"23 sets on trial. Only one got STOP." reads as a verdict.** The beat cuts out on the lone red tile, and the two promises (IT GOT ONE WRONG, AT THE END: THE JOB) land there as corner tags before the five steps. The tags must be rendered into the clips, in the top corners, clear of the seam caption band.
- **The five steps move faster and show progress.** Steps 1-3 take about 7 s with a 1/5 to 5/5 marker. The false alarm pays off the first tag between steps 4 and 5, so the coach arrives as the answer to "So who checks my AI?".
- **Each CTA appears once.** The gym-buddy send sits on the raw swing, and Comment FORM comes at the end with what they get in plain words.
- **The bridge line differs from the panel's example.** "This judge-and-coach check? That's the job." would contradict the posting, which says it is a model-training role and not a wrapper around a hosted LLM. "I check AI. This job trains it." keeps the personal stake and matches the posting, and it sets up the hired → evals → post-training series.
