## WHY THIS LANDS
- **The creator is the joke twice, and the AI is the straight man.** "Momentum did the rest" (2), "I cheat reps and fool my AI" (13) and "both agreed. Against me." (16) are the dry lines. "My crime? Turning to face the camera." stays the one real laugh, in silence at 1.0x.
- **The stake is physical and plain by 4 s.** "My legs stopped halfway" says what a short, swung rep is. The 122° and 53° figures stay on screen only, and there is no risk wording or slot anywhere.
- **Two promises are planted before the five steps.** Beat 6 says "One mistake" and shows IT GOT ONE WRONG plus the job-posting corner tag. The 1/5 to 5/5 marker holds on 4/5 through the lunge detour, so the false alarm reads as an interruption the viewer waits to resolve.
- **Each CTA appears once.** The gym-buddy send sits on the raw swing, and Comment FORM comes at the end with what they get, the AI coach prompt and the diagram.
- **Three changes to check.** These all came from fitting 61 s (MAIN) and 60 s (TRIAL), estimated at 2.8 words/s before the 1.15x speed-up.
  - **Bridge line:** I did not use "That's the job", because the posting itself says it is a model-training role, not a wrapper around a hosted LLM. "Checking AI is a skill now" bridges without claiming the analyser is that job.
  - **Merged beats:** "So how does it know?" is folded into step one, and the hook's loop closes in beat 17 alone.
  - **Moved on screen:** "Not a textbook", "Short, swung reps" and "It would have stopped me" are now on-screen text, not spoken.
