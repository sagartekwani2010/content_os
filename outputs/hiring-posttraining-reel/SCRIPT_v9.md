## Why this version makes the eval look necessary

- **The viewer approves before the eval speaks.** The stamp lands on beat 6 and cracks on beat 9; Diwali is first spoken on beat 10, under the checker's own verdict.
- **The struggle is now heard, not just shown.** The obvious fix fails three times, the "in waves" answer sounds smart and still fails, and one run admits it kept the rollout "because that is what you asked for".
- **Consistency is the payoff.** Three reds, three reds again, then three runs that all say "not yet", then 30 out of 30.
