## Teleprompter

1. Salesforce and Google are both cutting jobs, but still hiring people who can build with AI effectively.
2. Even OpenAI and Anthropic are doing the same.
3. So what's the one skill common across all these roles? Evals.
4. I'm a lead AI researcher, and writing evals is the main part of my job, for apps used by billions of people.
5. So what is an eval, and what does it actually mean? Let's see with a consulting example.
6. Imagine Swiggy's menu-to-cart conversion drops 30%. People are opening menus, but not adding anything to cart.
7. The business head blames the new delivery fee, which went live that same day.
8. You're called in as the consultant. What will you do?
9. You'll take the conversion data across days, put it into Claude, and ask for a slide with a recommendation.
10. It will give you a slide. But it can be completely wrong.
11. Why? Because a real analyst does much more before giving a recommendation. So how can AI do that? That's where evals come in.
12. An eval-driven AI system checks the whole journey first: people opening the app, visiting the menu, adding to cart, and placing orders.
13. And here's the twist: app opens, menu visits and orders are all going up. Only add-to-cart went down.
14. But you can't place an order without adding to cart. So people didn't stop adding. The app stopped counting.
15. The real reason: an Android update broke the add-to-cart tracking. The fee was never the problem.
16. That's the effective way to use AI as a consultant.
17. AI gives you speed. Evals give you speed you can trust, and that gets you ahead.
18. Comment EVALS to get all the evals I use for consulting.

## The transition you asked for (old points 9 → 10, now beats 13–15)

Instead of "all of it is increasing, and that helps you identify tracking", the reveal now comes in three short steps:
- **The twist:** "app opens, menu visits and orders are all going up. Only add-to-cart went down."
- **The logic anyone can repeat:** "you can't place an order without adding to cart. So people didn't stop adding. The app stopped counting."
- **The answer:** "an Android update broke the add-to-cart tracking. The fee was never the problem."

The viewer works it out a half-second before you say it, which is what makes it land.
