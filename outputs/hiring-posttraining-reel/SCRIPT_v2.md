## Why it is built this way

- **Rhythm:** fear (cuts) → hook (the job) → curiosity → number → widening (4 companies, ending on the biggest number)
  → the recap answers "why evals" → your own mistake → the rule → the tease answered → the series. Two pairs break the
  E/H alternation on purpose: 11-12 (the definition needs its setup) and 17-19 (payoff, series, CTA).
- **Re-hooks:** about 1/3 in (beat 8, $850K) and 2/3 in (beat 15, 6.9 vs 5.0).
- **Save asset:** beat 16, EXAMINER ≠ STUDENT. **Forward line:** beat 6 ("also in Bengaluru") for Indian engineers.
- **Your voice:** "So, out of curiosity", "actually", "literally", "So always…": phrases you use on camera.
