What's your number? (Run #1): mood board

Concept

A real evidence board, filmed like a dated lab log: paper cards, stickies and tape carry every beat, hand-drawn lines only annotate them, and the reel opens and closes on a "?" card at the lens.

Palette

#0E1116 background: top-panel backing, caption plate, letterbox behind screen recordings
#F5F1E8 paper white: cards, on-screen text, captions
#FFD23F sticky yellow: task stickies and the big numbers ([N], [TOTAL], [OLD_N])
#4CC9F0 chalk cyan: hand-drawn annotations over real footage (circle, arrow, loop)
#3DDC84 pass green: three ticks, the [FREED_WORK] sticky, [AI_TIME]
#FF4D4D fail red: the three crosses and the [FAILED_TASK] label only
#FFB86B warm amber: grade lift for the calm turn (scene 8) and the invitation (scene 10)

Type

Big numbers and main on-screen lines: heavy condensed uppercase sans, paper white, with the number itself in sticky yellow. Annotations (circle, arrow, loop words): marker-style handwriting in chalk cyan that matches the lettering on the real cards. Small-print disclosure lines: clean regular sans, one line, paper white at 70% opacity, placed directly under the main line. Captions: one line, low, white on a soft dark plate, above Instagram's bottom overlay. Physical cards: thick black chisel marker on matte white A5, with the "?" filling at least 60% of card height.

Texture

Real matte paper, marker ink and matte tape under soft even light. Overlays are a slightly wobbly 6-8 px hand-drawn line with a faint glow, drawn on in 8-12 frames, with no drop shadows and no 3D. Light film grain over everything so screen recordings and camera footage sit together. Screen recordings sit in a thin paper-white rounded frame on the dark background.

Music

Low minimal pulse bed (soft synth pulse, no melody, about 100 bpm) at the REEL_DEFAULTS.md level, which I have not checked. Silent under the opening question, enters on the card snap at 0:03. Thins to a single pulse for the confession (0:08.5). Level through the method and the older-model card, with no sting. Cuts out on the tape tap (0:27.5) and returns on the cut. Fullest and warmest for the loop (0:30.5-0:37.5). Drops to silence under "For now" (0:41.3). Returns warm on "Now your number" and resolves on the card push at 0:52.4.

SFX

Card snap (the flip), recorded on set, up front
Card slap (two cards into the slots)
Soft tick (three task phrases, three grades)
Sticky peel
Sticky press (soft thump)
Finger tap on tape (dry, with the music cut)
Tape rip (one pull, up front)
No whooshes, risers or bass impacts in this reel

Pacing

21 keyframes in 54.5 s, so a frame change roughly every 2.6 s, faster in the first 12 s (five changes) and around the reveal. Hard cuts only, and no title cards: the real cards do the section work, so the house whoosh and bass impact are not used. Three deliberate still points break the rhythm: the music-free question (0:00-0:03), the tap (0:27.5) and "For now" (0:41.3). The layout alternates full-frame creator, split and full-frame prop, so no layout holds longer than about 7 s. Split frames run 0:13.5-0:27.5, 0:30.5-0:37.5 and 0:47.2-0:50.3. The reel ends at 0:54.5, inside the 58 s limit carried over from the last draft.

References

A detective's evidence wall, but tidy: one column of notes, a dated header, and one item deliberately covered
A scoreboard number flip: one physical card turn that changes what the frame means
A lab notebook page: a date, a run number, tick and cross marks in three boxes, nothing decorative
A team task wall with sticky notes, where an empty slot reads as work that has moved
A teacher's flashcard held beside the face, question on one side and answer on the other
Chalk annotations over live footage, the way a sports analyst circles one player and draws one arrow

Risks

The screenplay text I received stops mid-row in section 5, so the remaining result-dependent alternates and the CTA note flagged for approval are not reflected here.
Every number and name is still a placeholder ([N], [TOTAL], [OLD_N], [TASK], [BEFORE_TIME], [AI_TIME], [FREED_WORK], [FAILED_TASK], [MODEL], [OLD_MODEL], [OLD_MODEL_DATE], [COMMON_TASK], [RUN2_DATE]). Write no card and build no overlay until the run is measured. The header strip must show the real run date.
If [N] is 0 or equals [TOTAL], KF02, KF11 and KF14-KF16 change to the screenplay's alternates. Decide before writing the cards.
The small-print disclosure lines run longer than six words and may be unreadable on a phone. Keep each to one line under the main text and repeat the detail in the caption.
The split frames show the same action from two angles. Shoot the inserts with a second locked phone, or match hand position exactly, or the top and bottom will visibly disagree.
The creator stands at a board here, not seated as in the house style. In split frames, crop the bottom half so the face still sits high and the caption line stays clear.
Glare on tape, cards and the laptop screen would hide the text that carries the payoff. Use matte tape and matte card, and check every close shot on the phone before moving on.
Handwriting that is too thin will not read. Use a chisel marker, write task names in three words or fewer, and test legibility at phone size.
The circle, arrow and loop must be tracked to the moving footage. A circle that slides off the [COMMON_TASK] row looks fake.
Three seconds of screen recording is too fast to read. Each recording needs one still, legible moment: the two panes side by side in KF10, and the dated Run card in KF19.
Show only the real recordings. Do not mock up the GPT screen, add a product logo graphic or rebuild the Run card in graphics. Remove employer and client data before the laptop recording.
The old-model card needs a verified release date, and 'one comparison' must stay on screen so [OLD_N] → [N] does not read as a trend.
The warm grade and full music in scene 8 must not tip into reassurance. No text, colour or sound should imply this keeps anyone safe.
'For now' only lands if the eyes are in frame. KF15 and KF16 must keep part of the face in the close shot, as in KF11.
The loop depends on KF21 matching KF01. Mark the floor, the tripod and the card position, and check both frames side by side in the edit.
There are no title cards, whooshes or bass impacts in this reel. Adding them for house consistency would fight the real-prop sound and the three silences.
I have not checked pace, caption or audio-level rules against production/REEL_DEFAULTS.md. Confirm the music level and the caption safe zone there before the edit.
