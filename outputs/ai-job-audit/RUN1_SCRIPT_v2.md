What changed from v1, and why:

- **Hook:** it now names the task; the "AI didn't take my job" frame is close to the saturated openers.
- **Beat 2:** "sitting duck" is gone, because it can imply laid-off people sat still. "I build AI" moves here, paired with vulnerability.
- **Beat 3:** "every task in my week" overclaimed; it is now "[TOTAL] tasks from my real week". The on-screen text says where the rubric is.
- **Beat 4:** "passed clean" is now the literal rule, "passed all three runs". Small text names the model and says you graded it.
- **Beat 5:** "One year." is cut unless the old model really is about a year old. The feeling is speed, not alarm.
- **Beat 6:** "AI's job now" overclaimed while the small text said you review it. The spoken line now says you check it, and [AI_TIME] includes that check.
- **Beat 10:** one ask instead of three; the next-reel tease moves to on-screen text.
- **Trial openings:** "the things I'm paid for" and "my job" overclaimed; both now say "my week".

## 1. Title, logline, arc

- **Title:** Run #1: AI vs my own week
- **Logline:** An AI engineer tests [TOTAL] real tasks from his own week against today's AI, shows the dated score, hands one task over, and promises to re-run it every month.
- **Emotion arc:** wry surprise → "he's exposed too, and so am I" → "I could do this" → respect for the proof → seeing the speed → curiosity about the taped task → calm agency → invitation.
