My Tuesday morning (Run #1): mood board

Concept

A real evidence board and a paper planner do the talking: one yellow sticky travels from planner to board, one strip of tape hides the task AI failed, and thin chalk-line overlays only point at what is physically there.

Palette

#0E1014 background: top-panel diagrams, text plates, letterbox behind screen recordings
#F4F1EA chalk white: line drawings, primary on-screen text, captions
#FFD23F sticky yellow accent: [TASK], big numbers, the handover, Run #2 outline
#3DDC84 pass green: ticks, 'pass', the green-ticked [TASK] row
#FFA94D amber: 'pass with edits', [E] count
#FF4D4D fail red: crosses, taped row, [FAILED_TASK] marks (never as a full-frame wash)
#5CC8FF cool blue: AI-output side of the screen recording, GPT phone outline
#C9B8A0 kraft tan: tape and paper tone to hold in the grade so real props stay warm, not grey

Type

Three tiers. (1) Big text and numbers: heavy condensed uppercase sans (Anton / Bebas Neue style), chalk white with the number or key word in sticky yellow, on a soft dark plate when over the bright board. (2) Annotations (circle labels, loop words, column labels): a hand-lettered marker face close to the creator's real handwriting, so overlays read as part of the board. (3) Small print (disclosures such as 'graded by me', 'measured once'): clean grotesk, sentence case, 70% opacity, one line only, never under about 40 px at 1440 wide. Captions: one line, low, above the bottom ~20% Instagram overlay, same grotesk, white on a thin dark plate. Never more than one big line plus one small line on screen at once. Caption size and position are still to be checked against REEL_DEFAULTS.md.

Texture

Real over drawn. Props: matte yellow stickies, matte kraft/masking tape, uncoated card stock, thick black marker, green and red markers for ticks and crosses. Overlays: 6-8 px chalk-white strokes with slight wobble, drawn on in 8-12 frames, no glow except a faint yellow halo on the Run #2 dashed outline in the last frame. Split top panels that are real footage get a thin hand-drawn chalk frame with rounded corners so they sit in the house style. Grade: whiteboard whites pulled down to warm off-white (about 85%) so cuts against the dark panels do not flash; light film grain at 5-8%; skin warm; no vignette.

Music

One low, steady pulse bed (soft muted pluck or felt-piano pulse over a sub drone, no drums, no risers), level per REEL_DEFAULTS.md (unchecked). Cue map: silent 0:00-0:01 so the peel leads; enters on 'now' (~0:02); stays low through scene 2; drops out for the half-second card hold (~0:18.5); thins to a single note under the look (~0:22, only if scene 5 survives); cuts out on the tape tap (0:31) and returns on the cut (0:34); fullest and warmest 0:34-0:40, held on 'And this slot?'; out completely for 'For now.' (~0:45.5-0:47); returns brighter at 0:47 and resolves on the nod (~0:55).

SFX

Sticky peel (real, close-mic'd, up front at 0:00)
Card slap on the board (real, scenes 4 and 5)
Sticky press with thumb rub (real, scene 6)
Fingertip tap on the taped row (real, dry, scene 7)
Fingertip tap on planner paper (real, scene 8)
Tape rip in one pull (real, loudest sound in the reel, scene 9)
Marker on paper (real, under the writing insert)
Soft tick x3 for pass / edits / fail (pen-click or marker-dot foley, quiet)
Room-tone bed for the three silences, so drop-outs do not sound like an export error
No whooshes, risers or bass impacts in this reel: the real prop sounds are the hits

Pacing

56 s, 21 keyframes, hard cuts only (the screenplay asks for no whooshes and no title cards; the physical Run #1 card and the tape do the job of section cards). The frame changes every 1.5-3.5 s and never repeats a layout more than twice in a row: full-frame creator (peel, punch-in) → split with board pan → full-frame card slap → split cards → split sticky then screen recording → full-frame taped row → split overhead planner → full-frame rip, close reveal, split insert, tight face → split GPT demo → full-frame close. Three deliberate silences carry the emotion: the half-second after the card (respect), the tap on the tape (curiosity) and 'For now.' (jolt). Overlays land on or just before the word; numbers appear big first and their small print follows 6-8 frames later. If scene 5 is cut, drop KF08 and pull every later keyframe 3 s earlier (runtime ~53 s).

Risks

Source gaps: the screenplay I was given is cut off in the hook map (the 0:55 row) and section 5 (the conditions for scene 5, the fallback opening and the freed-slot truth check) was not included. KF08, KF17 and the opening are designed from scenes 1-10 only.
REEL_DEFAULTS.md and the reel-script-writing skill were not checked upstream, so caption size, safe zones and music level here are house-style assumptions.
Unfilled placeholders: every number, task name, model name and date must come from the real run. A typeset number that does not match the handwritten card will read as fake.
Scene 5 is conditional. If it is cut, drop KF08 and retime everything after it by 3 s; do not leave a second card on the board in later shots.
Tape reveal: dry-erase ink lifts with tape, and thin tape can show ghost text under the key light. Use a paper strip or permanent marker under opaque matte tape, and test it on camera first.
Glare and legibility: glossy tape, cards and the whiteboard can blow out under the soft key. The date on the Run #1 card and the three paused rows must be readable on a phone, so use a thick marker and large handwriting.
Bright whiteboard against the dark house style: full-frame board shots can flash against the dark top panels. Pull the whites down in the grade and keep text on dark plates.
Text overload: big line, small print, captions and handwritten props can stack to four layers. Hold to one big line plus one small line, and never repeat as an overlay what is already readable on a prop in the same frame (KF07 is the one deliberate exception, for the date).
The on-screen text in KF07, KF08 and KF19 runs past six words once the required small print is counted. Keep the small print visibly secondary, or move it to the caption if it crowds the frame.
Split crop: the screenplay shoots vertical at 1x, but the bottom panel is roughly 9:8. Frame the face in the upper third with headroom for split scenes (or shoot those takes landscape), or the crop will cut the chin or the hands.
Standing bottom panels (scenes 3 and 10) depart from the seated house look. Keep them medium with the face high so the split still reads as the usual format.
Hand-drawn overlays on handheld footage: the circle on [COMMON_TASK] will slide if the pan is still moving. Pause the pan for the circle or motion-track it.
Screen recordings: tiny text in a half-height panel reads as noise, so zoom in and show less. Remove all employer and client data, and do not recreate or mock up any UI. The GPT demo must be a real run on a made-up example, and its Run card date must match the real one.
No added logos or product badges for [MODEL], [OLD_MODEL] or ChatGPT; show only what the real recordings and handwritten cards show.
'For now.' must be a jolt, not a threat: no red wash, no sting, no glitch effect. Silence and the tight frame are enough.
KF17 is only honest if the freed slot really goes to [FAILED_TASK]. If it does not, cut the insert and stay on his face.
Continuity is one-way: the sticky leaves the planner in scene 1 and lives on the board from scene 6, the Run #1 card stays up from scene 4, and the 'Run #2' outline is present from scene 3. Shooting out of the listed order will break it.
This reel has no title cards, whooshes or bass impacts (the screenplay asks for real prop sounds and hard cuts). If the real peel, slap and rip are recorded weakly, the reel will feel flat, so close-mic the wild tracks.
