## Which to shoot first

I would test these five, since they lead with a concrete task or number and work for a non-IT viewer:

- **#3:** the strongest visual proof, and it needs no context to understand.
- **#19:** the most sendable line.
- **#5:** any viewer can picture their own list.
- **#15:** it pairs the AI-engineer identity with a real loss, which removes the distance problem.
- **#28:** the only fitness hook that reads instantly and ties to Gym Jarvis.

Spoken lines and first frames can be mixed. For example, #19's spoken line works over #3's split screen.
