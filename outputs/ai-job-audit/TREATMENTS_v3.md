# Three treatments for the AI Job Audit reel (55 s)

All spoken lines are Hinglish, on-screen text is English, and every result is a placeholder until the real recordings exist.

## 1. "Meri job ka trial"

**Concept:** He puts his own job in the dock on camera, takes the verdict, then fights back live.

**Emotion curve**
- **0-10 s:** dread
- **10-20 s:** sting
- **20-30 s:** defiance
- **30-40 s:** relief
- **40-55 s:** power, handed to the viewer

**Hooks**
- **0 s (dread):** "Weekly status report. Har Monday mera ek ghanta. Aaj maine apni hi job ka audit kiya, camera on."
- **5 s (recognition):** The 8-role grid flashes. "CA ka GST recon, HR ka JD likhna, teacher ka question paper. Sabka ek task is list mein hai."
- **14 s (sting, confession):** "[MY_N] of [MY_TOTAL]. Sach bolun? Yeh dekh ke bura laga. Main AI engineer hoon."
- **26 s (defiance):** "Toh maine wahi kiya jo card ne bola. Abhi, aapke saamne."
- **38 s (surprise):** "Aur jo task 'Insaan chahiye' nikla, wahi meri asli job hai."
- **47 s (invitation):** "Ab aapki baari."

**Demo moments**
- **6-14 s:** Full-frame recording of him ticking tasks, with the ratings landing one by one.
- **14-18 s:** Same input run twice, same counts both times.
- **26-36 s:** He pastes the card's exact prompt and the status report appears, with a real stopwatch and [TIME_SAVED].
- **38-44 s:** The result card, zoomed on "Skill badhao" and "2-week experiment".

**Reason to use:** "2 minute mein aapke apne tasks ki list: kaunsa aaj AI ko dena hai, exact prompt ke saath. Koi roadmap yeh nahi deta. Comment AUDIT, job batana optional."

**Riskiest element:** The sting has to be real. If [MY_N] is low, or his reaction looks acted, the confession collapses.

## 2. "Do minute, ek call"

**Concept:** A real-time audit of a [FRIEND_ROLE] friend on a call, with a visible 2:00 countdown, building to the row that only a human can do.

**Emotion curve**
- **0-10 s:** nervous curiosity
- **10-20 s:** suspense
- **20-30 s:** discomfort
- **30-40 s:** dignity and warmth
- **40-55 s:** hope

**Hooks**
- **0 s (curiosity):** "Variance commentary. Mera dost har mahine yeh likhta hai. Maine usse call pe bola: 2 minute do, tumhari job audit karte hain."
- **11 s (suspense):** The rating hangs on [FRIEND_TASK_1]. "Ruko. Yeh wala dekho."
- **22 s (discomfort):** "[FRIEND_N] of [FRIEND_TOTAL]. Call pe do second koi kuch nahi bola." Use this line only if the silence really happens.
- **32 s (pride):** "Phir yeh row aayi: [FRIEND_TASK_HUMAN]. 'Insaan chahiye.' Client ka trust AI nahi kama sakta."
- **43 s (hope):** "Usne call pe hi pehla task AI ko de diya."

**Demo moments**
- **3-22 s:** Screen share of the friend's ticks and the live ratings, including the "AI draft, human sign-off" flag. The friend's face and name are never shown; only their real, unscripted voice or reaction is used.
- **24-27 s:** The audit is re-run and gives the same counts.
- **32-38 s:** The dignity row, held on screen.
- **43-49 s:** The friend's result card, then the 8-role grid.

**Reason to use:** "Aapke boss, aapka appraisal, koi YouTube video yeh nahi batayega: aapke 11 tasks mein se kaunse aapke hi rehne wale hain. 2 minute, no login. Comment AUDIT, job batana optional."

**Riskiest element:** The whole reel depends on an unscripted reaction. If the friend is flat, or the consented audio is unusable, there is no reel.

## 3. "Main haar gaya"

**Concept:** He races AI on his own Monday task, loses badly, and the loss turns out to be the win.

**Emotion curve**
- **0-10 s:** adrenaline
- **10-20 s:** humiliation, played for humour
- **20-30 s:** the turn
- **30-40 s:** ambition
- **40-55 s:** urgency for the viewer

**Hooks**
- **0 s (adrenaline):** A split screen with two stopwatches. "Weekly status report. Main versus AI. Go."
- **6 s (recognition):** While he types: "Aapka version? GST recon, branch MIS, deck formatting." Role chips slide in.
- **12 s (shock, humour):** The AI finishes while he is on line two. "Khatam. Main abhi 'Hi team' pe hoon."
- **23 s (reframe):** "Lekin maine yeh task choose kaise kiya? Guess nahi kiya."
- **35 s (ambition):** "[TIME_SAVED] har hafte wapas. Card ne bola isse yahan lagao: [MY_SKILL]."
- **46 s (urgency):** "Aapki race kaunse task pe hai?"

**Demo moments**
- **0-14 s:** The real race, unedited, with the real stopwatch.
- **23-32 s:** The audit that picked the task, showing ratings, [MY_N] of [MY_TOTAL], and the "AI ko do" prompt he used.
- **32-35 s:** Same input, same counts.
- **35-42 s:** The "Skill badhao" and "2-week experiment" card.
- **46-50 s:** The 8-role grid.

**Reason to use:** "Sabko pata hai AI fast hai. Yeh 2 minute mein batata hai aapka kaunsa task race mein daalna hai, aur kaunsa kabhi nahi. Comment AUDIT, job batana optional."

**Riskiest element:** The race can read as "AI beats humans" doom. The 23 s turn must land hard, and the AI's report must be good enough to survive his on-camera check.

## Recommendation

Shoot Treatment 1 as the spine and borrow Treatment 3's stopwatch for the 26-36 s fight-back. It depends only on footage he controls, and the confession is the strongest honest emotion available.

Treatment 2 has the highest ceiling for sends, but only if the call delivers. Record the call anyway and decide after hearing it.
