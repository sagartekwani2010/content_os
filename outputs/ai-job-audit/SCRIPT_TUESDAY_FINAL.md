Three panel fixes need your sign-off:

- **The freed time now goes to the failed task.** The empty planner slot is filled with [FAILED_TASK] in the reveal, so scenes 8 and 9 are one move and [FREED_WORK] is gone. This is only usable if it is true (see section 5).

## 1. Title, logline, arc

- **Title:** Run #1: AI vs my own week
- **Logline:** An AI engineer peels one task off his own planner, shows AI's dated score against [TOTAL] tasks from his week, gives the freed slot to the one task AI failed, and hands viewers a free GPT to run their own Run #1.
- **Emotion arc:** wry surprise → "he's exposed, and he's asking me" → "that's my kind of task" → respect for a dated number → quiet unease → "he acted on it" → curiosity about the taped task → calm → payoff and a small jolt on "For now" → "I can do this tonight".

**Why this version works**

- **Hook:** five placeholder words, a named task and a physical peel. [TASK] must be one a non-coder recognises.
- **Non-IT doorway:** scene 2 now turns to the viewer ("Is yours?") while their tasks are on screen, and scene 3 says one aloud.
- **Back-test:** it says "an older AI" by default, shows two cards with no arrow, and is cut entirely if the comparison isn't clean.
- **Freed time:** it goes to the task AI failed, so the line is a lesson about where human time moves and not a personal win.
- **CTA:** one action (comment RUN). "Bring the one it failed" is safe to post in front of colleagues and is a point of pride.
- **Runtime:** 111 placeholder words, timed at 2.4 words per second so the holds and "unhurried" directions are affordable.
