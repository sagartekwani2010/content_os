# Reel 1: "Run #1" (final Tuesday script)

I did not open the `reel-script-writing` skill or `production/REEL_DEFAULTS.md` in this session, so check pace, caption and audio-level rules against them before you approve the beat sheet. Nothing here is saved to the repo yet.

## 1. Title, logline, arc

- **Title:** Run #1: AI vs my own week
- **Logline:** An AI engineer peels one task off his own planner, shows the dated score of AI against [TOTAL] real tasks from his week, keeps one failed task under tape, and hands viewers a free GPT to run their own Run #1.
- **Emotion arc:** wry surprise → "he's exposed too" → "that's my kind of task" → respect for a dated number → unease at the one-year gap → "he acted on it" → curiosity about the taped task → calm → small jolt on "For now" → "I'm in".

**Why this version works**

- **Hook:** the spoken hook is five words and names a task, so it fits in 3 s at a normal pace.
- **Non-IT doorway:** it now appears twice, as text in scene 2 and as a legible, spoken task in scene 3.
- **Back-test:** it uses plain words, discloses the prompt bias, and claims one comparison, not a trend.
- **Scene 8:** it promises the monthly run in the future tense and names one concrete thing you did with the time.
- **CTA:** it is one action (comment RUN), and "Bring your number" makes running the GPT feel like joining Run #2.
- **Drama:** it comes from physical moments (the peel, the empty slot, the tape, the empty Run #2 outline) and silence, not from claims.

## 2. Screenplay

These hold for every scene unless a scene says otherwise:

- **Wardrobe:** fitted plain dark tee, no logo.
- **Camera:** phone main lens (1x), vertical 9:16, locked on a tripod at eye level.
- **Lighting:** one soft key (window or softbox) 45° camera-left, with a white bounce on the right. Light the whiteboard evenly and check for glare on tape and cards.
- **Layout:** "Full" is full frame. "Top half" puts the visual in the top half, with you speaking to lens in the bottom half.
- **Sound:** a low, steady pulse bed under the voice at the level set in `REEL_DEFAULTS.md`. The SFX are the real sounds recorded on the shoot (peel, card slap, marker, tape).
- **Board:** a dashed empty outline labelled "Run #2" is on the right of the board from scene 3, and nobody mentions it until scene 10.

### Scene 1: The peel (0:00-0:03)

- **Set:** desk. Paper weekly planner held at chest height, with a yellow sticky reading "[TASK]" in the [DAY] morning slot.
- **Camera:** full frame, medium close-up, static. Face and planner are both sharp in the first frame.
- **Lighting:** standard.
- **Action:** you peel the sticky off the slot in one move as you speak. The slot is visibly empty.
- **Spoken:** "AI does my [TASK] now."
- **On-screen text:** My [DAY] morning → AI
- **Visual:** the real planner and sticky, no graphic.
- **Viewer emotion:** wry surprise.
- **Delivery:** dry half-smile, normal pace, eyes on the lens after the peel.
- **Continuity:** you start seated and still, and end with the sticky in your right hand.
- **Sound:** no music for the first second; the peel sound is up front. Music enters on "now".
- **Transition out:** hard cut on the last word.

### Scene 2: Not safe either (0:03-0:07)

- **Set:** same seat. The planner is lowered and the sticky is still in your hand.
- **Camera:** full frame, slightly tighter close-up (punch in about 10% in the edit).
- **Lighting:** standard.
- **Action:** none. Stay still.
- **Spoken:** "I build AI for a living. That doesn't make me layoff-proof."
- **On-screen text:** GST filing? Lesson plans? Follow-ups? (appears at 0:03, one phrase at a time)
- **Visual:** face only.
- **Viewer emotion:** "he's exposed too, and he means me."
- **Delivery:** level and straight to lens, with a small breath before "That doesn't". No fear and no bravado.
- **Continuity:** energy drops one notch from scene 1. The sticky is visible.
- **Sound:** music stays low. No SFX.
- **Transition out:** cut on "So" to the board.

### Scene 3: The method (0:07-0:15)

- **Set:** whiteboard with [TOTAL] handwritten task rows and three tick boxes per row. The rubric sheet is taped on the left. One row is covered with opaque tape and has three red crosses.
- **Camera:** top half. The top is a slow handheld pan down the board that pauses on three legible rows, one of them [COMMON_TASK]. The bottom is you standing by the board in a medium shot.
- **Lighting:** standard.
- **Action:** you count three grades on your fingers.
- **Spoken:** "So I gave AI [TOTAL] tasks from my real week, like [COMMON_TASK]. Three runs each: pass, edits, or fail."
- **On-screen text:** [TOTAL] tasks · 3 runs · pass / pass with edits / fail (small: rubric + outputs in caption)
- **Visual:** the real board. A hand-drawn circle animates around the [COMMON_TASK] row as you say it.
- **Viewer emotion:** "that's fair, and that's my kind of task."
- **Delivery:** brisk and clear, with a slight lift on [COMMON_TASK].
- **Continuity:** you are standing, and the sticky is in your hand or pocket and out of the way.
- **Sound:** a soft tick on each of the three grades.
- **Transition out:** cut as your hand lifts the score card.

### Scene 4: The dated number (0:15-0:20)

- **Set:** board. A score card is handwritten "Run #1 · 18 Nov 2026 · [N] of [TOTAL]".
- **Camera:** full frame, medium shot, static. The card ends at eye level.
- **Lighting:** standard.
- **Action:** you press the card onto the board above the list and take your hand away. Hold for half a second.
- **Spoken:** "Run one, eighteenth November. [N] of [TOTAL] passed all three runs."
- **On-screen text:** Run #1 · 18 Nov 2026 · [N] of [TOTAL] (small: [E] with edits · [F] fail · [MODEL] · graded by me)
- **Visual:** the real card.
- **Viewer emotion:** respect for a real, dated number.
- **Delivery:** flat and factual. Don't sell it.
- **Continuity:** the card stays up in every later board shot.
- **Sound:** card slap. The music drops out for the half-second hold and then returns.
- **Transition out:** cut.

### Scene 5: A year ago (0:20-0:24)

- **Set:** board. A second, smaller card reads "[OLD_MODEL] · [OLD_MODEL_DATE] · [OLD_N] of [TOTAL]".
- **Camera:** top half. The top is a close shot of both cards as your finger moves from old to new. The bottom is your face.
- **Lighting:** standard.
- **Action:** you stick the old card to the left of Run #1 and slide your finger across.
- **Spoken:** "AI from a year ago, same tasks: [OLD_N]."
- **On-screen text:** [OLD_N] → [N] (small: same prompts · one comparison)
- **Visual:** the real cards. A hand-drawn arrow animates from old to new.
- **Viewer emotion:** quiet unease at the gap.
- **Delivery:** even, then a half-second look at the lens after [OLD_N]. Say nothing more.
- **Continuity:** both cards stay up.
- **Sound:** the music thins to a single note under the look.
- **Transition out:** cut.

### Scene 6: The handover (0:24-0:31)

- **Set:** board. The green-ticked [TASK] row.
- **Camera:** top half. The top is a close shot of your hand pressing the sticky onto the row for 2 s, then a screen recording for about 3 s. The bottom is your face.
- **Lighting:** standard.
- **Action:** the sticky from scene 1 goes onto the [TASK] row.
- **Spoken:** "[TASK] took me [BEFORE_TIME]. Now AI does it, I check it: [AI_TIME]."
- **On-screen text:** [TASK]: [BEFORE_TIME] → [AI_TIME] (small: includes my check)
- **Visual:** the real screen recording, with your old version on the left and the AI output on the right, both scrolling, and employer or client data removed.
- **Viewer emotion:** "he acted on it."
- **Delivery:** a little rueful on the first sentence and plain on the second.
- **Continuity:** the sticky is now on the board for the rest of the reel.
- **Sound:** sticky press. No whoosh on the screen recording.
- **Transition out:** cut to the red row.

### Scene 7: The taped fail (0:31-0:34)

- **Set:** board. The taped row with three red crosses.
- **Camera:** full frame, close on the row. Your finger and part of your face are in frame.
- **Lighting:** standard.
- **Action:** one tap on the tape, then you look at the lens.
- **Spoken:** "This one failed. All three runs."
- **On-screen text:** Failed 3 of 3 · remember this one
- **Visual:** the real row.
- **Viewer emotion:** curiosity.
- **Delivery:** lower voice, with a held look after "runs".
- **Continuity:** the tape is untouched.
- **Sound:** the music cuts out on the tap and returns on the cut.
- **Transition out:** cut to the desk.

### Scene 8: The loop (0:34-0:42)

- **Set:** desk and planner. The [DAY] morning slot is empty.
- **Camera:** top half. The top is an overhead close shot of the planner slot. The bottom is your face, seated.
- **Lighting:** standard. Check there is no hand shadow on the overhead shot.
- **Action:** you write "[FREED_WORK]" into the empty slot.
- **Spoken:** "Every month, I'll re-run it. What newly passes, I hand over. This week, that time went to [FREED_WORK]."
- **On-screen text:** Measure → hand over → re-measure
- **Visual:** the real planner. The three words appear one at a time as a hand-drawn loop.
- **Viewer emotion:** calm. He has a plan, and it isn't a pitch.
- **Delivery:** steady and warm, and not rushed. The last sentence is specific and not proud.
- **Continuity:** seated, with the same planner as scene 1.
- **Sound:** marker on paper. The music is at its fullest here.
- **Transition out:** cut on the last pen stroke.

### Scene 9: The reveal (0:42-0:47)

- **Set:** board. The taped row.
- **Camera:** full frame. It starts on your face and hand and ends close on the row (two shots, or one push-in).
- **Lighting:** standard.
- **Action:** you peel the tape in one pull. Hold on the name for half a second.
- **Spoken:** "The one it failed? [FAILED_TASK]. Still mine. For now."
- **On-screen text:** [FAILED_TASK] ✗ ✗ ✗
- **Visual:** the real row.
- **Viewer emotion:** payoff, then a small jolt.
- **Delivery:** small smile on "Still mine". The smile goes on "For now", and you hold the look.
- **Continuity:** stick the used tape on the board edge so your hands are free.
- **Sound:** the tape rip is up front, and the music is out for "For now."
- **Transition out:** cut on a beat of silence.

### Scene 10: Your turn (0:47-0:56)

- **Set:** board behind you. The Run #1 card is visible, with the empty dashed "Run #2" outline beside it.
- **Camera:** top half for the GPT recording (about 3 s), then full-frame medium close-up for the last two lines. The last frame is your nod with the empty outline in view.
- **Lighting:** standard.
- **Action:** you point down at the comments on "Comment RUN" and tap the empty outline on "Run two".
- **Spoken:** "Your turn. Comment RUN, I'll DM you a free GPT that tests your tasks. Run two, [RUN2_DATE]. Bring your number."
- **On-screen text:** Comment RUN → I DM you the GPT · Run #2: [RUN2_DATE], post your number (small: also link in bio)
- **Visual:** a real screen recording of the Run #1 GPT attempting a dummy non-IT task, then the dated Run card appearing.
- **Viewer emotion:** invited, "I'm in".
- **Delivery:** friendly and direct. "Bring your number" is a challenge with a smile. End on a nod.
- **Continuity:** standing, with open hands.
- **Sound:** the music resolves on the nod. No SFX.
- **Transition out:** hold the empty outline for half a second, then end.

## 7. Pre-shoot measurement checklist

**The run**

- [ ] Write your full weekly task list and fix [TOTAL] before running anything.
- [ ] Write the rubric with one line per grade and the rule for turning three runs into one grade. The script assumes a pass is three of three.
- [ ] Run each task three times on today's model. Save the prompts, the outputs, the model name and version as [MODEL], and the date.
- [ ] Count [N], [E] and [F], and confirm they sum to [TOTAL].
- [ ] Run the same prompts on the older model for [OLD_N], and note [OLD_MODEL] and [OLD_MODEL_DATE].
- [ ] Pick [COMMON_TASK] from the real list: something a CA, teacher or bank RM also does.
- [ ] Pick [TASK]: a three-of-three pass that is really handed over. Note its real day and slot.
- [ ] Time [BEFORE_TIME] doing it yourself and [AI_TIME] including the prompt and your check.
- [ ] Pick [FAILED_TASK] and confirm its fail count.
- [ ] Write down what you actually did with the freed time this week as [FREED_WORK].
- [ ] Fix [RUN2_DATE] and put it in your calendar.

**The GPT**

- [ ] Build it so the viewer lists 3 to 5 weekly tasks, it attempts each on dummy or sanitised input, and the viewer grades each pass / pass with edits / fail.
- [ ] Make the output a dated Run card with the number, the task to hand over and its prompt, the task that stayed human, and a Run #2 date.
- [ ] Have it tell users not to paste employer or client data.
- [ ] End the card with "Post your number on the Run #1 reel."
- [ ] Test it end to end in a free ChatGPT account with at least three non-IT task sets.
- [ ] Set up and test the comment-RUN-to-DM delivery from a second account, and put the link in your bio.

**The caption must carry**

- [ ] The rubric and a link to the prompts and outputs.
- [ ] [MODEL] and [OLD_MODEL] with dates.
- [ ] "Graded by me."
- [ ] "Same prompts on both models, written on today's model, one comparison."
- [ ] A line saying the GPT makes one attempt per task on dummy input and the viewer grades it.
