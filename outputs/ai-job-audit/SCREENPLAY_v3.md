# SCREENPLAY v3: "Meri job ka trial" (55 s)

## 1. Logline + promise
He loses a race to AI on his own Monday task, puts his whole job on trial, takes the verdict, and fights back live.

**Promise:** in 2 minutes you know which of your tasks to hand to AI today, and which stay yours.

## 4. Screenplay

**S1 · 0-3 s · Race**
- **SHOT:** Full-frame split. His doc on the left, AI on the right, two real stopwatches.
- **SPOKEN:** "Weekly status report. Main versus AI. Go."
- **TEXT:** ME vs AI · Weekly status report
- **DEMO:** The real race, unedited.
- **EMOTION:** Adrenaline.
- **DELIVERY:** Fast, clipped, eyes on the screen.
- **CONTINUITY:** Cold in; out leaning forward.
- **SFX:** Stopwatch ticks, no music.

**S2 · 3-5 s · Loss**
- **SHOT:** Same split. The AI report is complete; his side reads "Hi team".
- **SPOKEN:** "Khatam. Main abhi 'Hi team' pe hoon."
- **TEXT:** AI: [AI_TIME]
- **EMOTION:** Humour.
- **DELIVERY:** Deadpan to lens, hands off the keyboard.
- **CONTINUITY:** Leaning in; out sitting back.
- **SFX:** Ding, then music in.

**S3 · 5-11 s · Grid**
- **SHOT:** Standard split, grid on top and him below.
- **SPOKEN:** "Isliye maine apni poori job audit ki. Aapka task bhi yahan hai: GST recon, branch MIS, question paper. Student? Target job chuno."
- **TEXT:** Eight chips at about 0.6 s each: IT status report · CA GST recon (CA sign-off) · Finance variance commentary · Consulting deck formatting · Marketing ad copy variants · HR JD likhna · Bank branch MIS · Teacher question paper. Then the "Student? Target job" toggle.
- **DEMO:** The tool's role picker.
- **EMOTION:** Recognition.
- **DELIVERY:** Warm, pointing up at the chips.
- **CONTINUITY:** Sat back; out neutral.
- **SFX:** A soft pop per chip.

**S4 · 11-18 s · Trial**
- **SHOT:** Full-frame phone recording.
- **SPOKEN (VO):** "Role: AI engineer. Apne hafte ke tasks tick kiye. Ab rating dekho."
- **TEXT:** The tool's own labels.
- **DEMO:** Ticks on [MY_TASK_*], then the ratings land one by one: "AI kar leta hai", "AI madad karta hai", "Insaan chahiye".
- **EMOTION:** Dread.
- **DELIVERY:** Slower and quieter.
- **CONTINUITY:** VO only.
- **SFX:** Music drops low, one tick per rating.

**S5 · 18-23 s · Verdict**
- **SHOT:** Split, the count on top and his face below.
- **SPOKEN:** Use the take that matches the real number.
  - If high: "AI engineer hoon, phir bhi [MY_N] of [MY_TOTAL]. Sach bolun, bura laga."
  - If low: "Socha tha zyada niklega. [MY_N] of [MY_TOTAL]."
- **TEXT:** [MY_N] of [MY_TOTAL] tasks
- **DEMO:** The count on the result card.
- **EMOTION:** Sting.
- **DELIVERY:** One breath of silence, eyes down, then to lens. No smile.
- **CONTINUITY:** Neutral in; out still.
- **SFX:** Music cuts for 0.5 s.

**S6 · 23-26 s · Proof**
- **SHOT:** Split, with two runs side by side on top.
- **SPOKEN:** "Dobara chalaya. Same input, same count. Yeh guess nahi hai."
- **TEXT:** Run 1 = Run 2
- **DEMO:** The identical counts from both runs.
- **EMOTION:** Trust.
- **DELIVERY:** Flat and factual.
- **CONTINUITY:** Still; out with chin up.
- **SFX:** Double click.

**S7 · 26-34 s · Fight-back**
- **SHOT:** Full-frame screen recording, then a before/after split.
- **SPOKEN:** "Toh maine wahi kiya jo card ne bola. [MY_TASK_2], card ka exact prompt. Pehle [BEFORE_TIME]. Ab [TIME_SAVED] wapas, checking meri."
- **TEXT:** BEFORE [BEFORE_TIME] · AFTER, with the stopwatch
- **DEMO:** "AI ko do" on the card, copy the prompt, paste it, the output appears, and he edits one line. Last week's manual version sits beside the AI draft.
- **EMOTION:** Defiance.
- **DELIVERY:** Energy up, faster.
- **CONTINUITY:** Chin up; out leaning in.
- **SFX:** Music returns with a beat, plus a whoosh.

**S8 · 34-42 s · Dignity row**
- **SHOT:** Split, the friend's card on top with no name.
- **SPOKEN:** "Mera dost, [FRIEND_ROLE]. [FRIEND_N] of [FRIEND_TOTAL]. Lekin yeh row dekho: [FRIEND_TASK_HUMAN]. Client ka trust AI nahi kama sakta."
- **Optional:** The friend's real line, [FRIEND_AUDIO], for 2 s.
- **TEXT:** AI draft, human sign-off · Insaan chahiye
- **DEMO:** The sign-off flag, then the human row held for 2 s.
- **EMOTION:** Pride.
- **DELIVERY:** Softer, slower, a small smile.
- **CONTINUITY:** Leaning in; out open.
- **SFX:** Music warms, with no effects.

**S9 · 42-47 s · Reason**
- **SHOT:** Split, card on top.
- **SPOKEN:** "Boss, appraisal, YouTube: koi nahi batayega aapke kaunse tasks aapke hi rahenge. Yeh batata hai. 2 minute."
- **TEXT:** Skill badhao: [MY_SKILL] · 2-week experiment
- **DEMO:** The card scrolls to those two sections.
- **EMOTION:** Power.
- **DELIVERY:** Direct, counting on his fingers.
- **CONTINUITY:** Open; out pointing at the lens.
- **SFX:** Music builds.

**S10 · 47-55 s · Ask**
- **SHOT:** His prompt full-frame for 3 s so it can be screenshotted, then him full-frame.
- **SPOKEN:** "No login, Hindi mein bhi. Comment AUDIT, job batana optional. Aur usko bhejo jiska Monday report mein jaata hai."
- **TEXT:** Comment AUDIT · Free · No login · No names, clients or company
- **DEMO:** The exact prompt, readable.
- **EMOTION:** Invitation.
- **DELIVERY:** Smiling and unhurried.
- **CONTINUITY:** Ends on a held look.
- **SFX:** Music resolves, with a final tick.

## 6. Why they run it, why they send it

**Run:**
- It rates their own tasks, where a roadmap gives generic advice.
- The exact prompt is ready to use today.
- It takes 2 minutes, with no login, in Hindi, Hinglish or English, and voice works.
- It gives counts, with no scary percentage, and the same input gives the same result.
- It shows what stays human.

**Send:**
- The named recipient: "jiska Monday report mein jaata hai".
- The grid lets a viewer tag a CA, HR or teacher friend.
- The dignity row is safe to forward because it insults no profession.
- The prompt is worth a screenshot.
