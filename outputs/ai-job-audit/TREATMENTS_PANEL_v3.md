## Winner: T1, rebuilt

T1 is the only treatment where the tool sits at the centre of the story and all the footage is under his control. Run is the metric that drives AUDIT comments, and T1 leads it. Its weaknesses are the static hook and a confession that relates mostly to tech viewers. Both can be fixed with parts of the other two treatments.

**Take from T3:**
- The split-screen stopwatch as a 0–3 s cold open.
- "Main abhi 'Hi team' pe hoon." It is the best laugh across all three treatments.
- "…aur kaunsa kabhi nahi," which reframes speed into judgement.

**Take from T2:**
- A real second voice in a non-tech role.
- The dignity row with "Client ka trust AI nahi kama sakta."
- The "AI draft, human sign-off" flag on screen, which is what the CA and bank viewers need.
- The "Aapke boss, aapka appraisal…" reason-to-use line, which is the strongest of the three.

## 5 changes that lift all 12

1. **Open with the race, then call it an audit.** At 0 s, show the split screen, "Weekly status report. Main vs AI. Go." At 3 s, cut to "Hi team…" as the AI finishes. At 5 s: "Isliye maine apni poori job audit ki." This keeps T3's stop rate without its doom read, because the tool arrives at 5 s instead of 23 s.

2. **Write the sting for both outcomes before he knows [MY_N].** If the number is high, he says "AI engineer hoon, phir bhi [MY_N] of [MY_TOTAL]." If it is low, he says "Socha tha zyada niklega." Only an honest reaction works on the strategy manager and the IB associate, and a backup script prevents faking one.

3. **Give the 8-role grid 5 seconds.** Show each chip for about 0.6 s with its own task word, and add a 1-s "Student? Target job choose karo" toggle. The bank RM, the teacher and the BTech student currently drop off because their role flashes by too fast.

4. **Put the friend's dignity row in at 34–42 s.** Show [FRIEND_ROLE]'s card with the human sign-off flag, hold the "Insaan chahiye" row, and play the friend's real voice for one line. If the audio is flat, use the card alone, with no reaction implied. This brings in T2's warmth and send trigger without making the reel depend on the call.

5. **Close on a concrete action plus a send cue.** Show his "AI ko do" prompt full-frame long enough to screenshot. Then say "2 minute, no login, Hindi mein bhi. Comment AUDIT, job batana optional. Aur usko bhejo jiska Monday report mein jaata hai." "Ab aapki baari" doesn't tell the viewer what to do. A named recipient lifts sends for every persona.
