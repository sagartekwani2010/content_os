## Role tiles and templates

**Finance**
- **Their four tasks:** "month-end variance commentary", "MIS deck banana", "comps / model update", "earnings note first draft".
- **Accept as "AI kar leta hai":** MIS formatting, commentary first draft, comps data pull, earnings summary.
- **Insulting or wrong:** rating forecast or valuation model building (assumptions are judgment) or the final investment view as AI work.
- **Regulated / sign-off:** research reports (SEBI RA, compliance sign-off), board and investor numbers, audited figures, and MNPI, which must never be pasted into the tool.

**Consulting**
- **Their four tasks:** "deck formatting", "desk research / market sizing first cut", "interview notes synthesise karna", "client status mail / minutes".
- **Accept:** formatting, notes, minutes, first-cut research.
- **Insulting or wrong:** storyline, the recommendation, and workshop facilitation as AI work. Market sizing as "AI kar leta hai" is wrong because the numbers get hallucinated.
- **Regulated / sign-off:** audit workpapers and opinions (Big 4 statutory), tax filings, and client NDAs. The "client ka naam mat likho" warning matters most here.

**Marketing**
- **Their four tasks:** "ad copy variations", "weekly ROAS / performance report", "agency or creative brief", "competitor creative tracking".
- **Accept:** copy variants, the report, first-draft briefs.
- **Insulting or wrong:** consumer insight, positioning and budget allocation as AI work.
- **Regulated / sign-off:** ASCI claim substantiation, FSSAI and health claims, and legal approval of packaging and ad claims.

## Do these groups need something different?

- **Hook:** They don't need a separate hook. They need a named task in the hook instead of a bare count, because "4 of 11" is abstract to everyone. The hook should be "Mera weekly report AI already likhta hai."
- **Example friend:** Make the friend a finance or CA person, such as an FP&A analyst or a CA. That one role covers personas 2, 7 and 8, and finance is among the most AI-exposed groups, so the friend's result carries tension. Save consulting and marketing for Reel 2.
- **Language:**
  - Keep the speech in Hinglish; it works for 1, 2, 6, 9 and 11.
  - Put task names in English on screen, because that is how everyone says them ("variance commentary", not a translation).
  - Keep on-screen text English-led. Finance and consulting in Mumbai and Gurgaon read Hinglish captions as less credible.

## What breaks

- **The count:** It is unanchored and self-rated, and there is no visible proof that it is consistent.
- **Tiles:** They miss finance, consulting and marketing. The bank tile has the wrong task for an RM, and the teacher is excluded by the "desk job" caption.
- **Public CTA:** "AUDIT + your job" asks senior professionals to post their job title publicly. Many won't.
- **Platform:** A custom GPT login loses 4, 5 and some of 8 and 10.
- **Gym analogy:** It spends about 3 s on the creator's world, not the viewer's.

## Top 5 fixes (stays at or under 55 s)

1. **Hook on a task, count second.** "Main AI banata hoon. Mera weekly status report AI already likhta hai: 11 mein se 4 tasks." This costs no time.
2. **Replace the four tiles with a 2-second grid of eight tiles.** Each tile shows one role and one task in their words: IT "status report", CA "GST recon (CA sign-off)", Finance "variance commentary", Consulting "deck formatting", Marketing "ad copy variants", HR "JD likhna", Bank "branch MIS", Teacher "question paper". Change the caption to "Kisi bhi job ke liye". This saves about 6 s.
3. **Show proof of consistency for about 2 s.** Show the same template run twice with identical counts, captioned "Fixed rubric. Har baar same." This is the main trust lever for 7–10, so ship it as the web form and say "no login".
4. **Give the friend's audit a dignity row.** Show one "AI kar leta hai" task and one "insaan chahiye" task, such as "client ko samjhana", so a viewer would forward it to the friend without it reading as an insult. Cut the gym analogy to pay for the extra time.
5. **Lower the comment bar.** "Comment AUDIT. Job batana optional, DM bhi kar sakte ho." Keep the auto-DM trigger on "AUDIT". Seniors will still opt in, and Reel 2 research comes from DMs.
