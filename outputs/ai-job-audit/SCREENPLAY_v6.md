# SCREENPLAY v6: "My job on trial" (55 s)

## 1. Logline + promise
AI finishes his Monday report while he is still on "Hi team", so he puts his whole job on trial, takes the verdict twice, hands one task over, and finds the row that stays his.

**Promise:** you see which tasks to hand to AI today, and which stay yours.

## 6. Why they run it, why they send it

**Run:**
- Six roles hear their own task in the first 12 s.
- The count survives a rerun on screen, so it reads as a rule and not a mood.
- The before/after is a measured task, with his edit shown.
- Two real cards each show a row that stays human.
- The ask is one action: free, no login, comment AUDIT.

**Send:**
- The send line names three friends, which prompts a tag.
- The human rows make it safe to forward, since they insult no profession.
- The "Hi team" laugh is worth sharing on its own.
