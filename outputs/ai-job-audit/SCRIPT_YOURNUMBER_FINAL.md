# Reel 1: "What's your number?" (Run #1, final pass)

Every panel fix is applied. The only one I adapted is "comment your guess", which I merged into the RUN comment so the CTA stays one action; that is flagged at the end for your approval.

I did not open `AGENTS.md`, the `reel-script-writing` skill or `production/REEL_DEFAULTS.md` in this session. Check pace, caption and audio-level rules, and the 58 s limit carried over from the last draft, against them before you approve the beat sheet. Nothing is saved to the repo.

## 1. Title, logline, arc

- **Title:** What's your number? (Run #1)
- **Logline:** An AI engineer asks how many of your tasks AI can do, flips a card to show his own dated number, and hands you a free GPT to get yours.
- **Emotion arc:** curiosity → "he means my job" → "he's exposed too" → trust in a dated number → "it moved" → "he acted" → curiosity about the taped task → calm → jolt on "For now" → "I want my number".

**Why this version works**

- **The viewer's number is the open loop.** A blank "YOU: ?" card enters at 0:07, sits beside his score all reel, and comes back to the lens in the last frame.
- **The hook is a question about them.** The flip now lands inside 3 s.
- **The non-IT doorway is spoken and wider.** "GST reconciliation, lesson plans, client decks" covers CAs, teachers, and the consulting, banking and brand viewers who scored lowest.
- **Nothing is claimed that he did not measure.** The lines are "AI drafts it, I check it", "an older AI", and "a dummy version of each task".
- **The CTA has an easy rung and a real rung.** The guess takes five seconds, and the GPT gives the real number to post.
- **The props do the drama.** The card flip, the gap left on the board, the tape and the blank card carry it without adjectives.
- **The last frame mirrors the first.** Both are a "?" card at the lens, so a rewatch loops cleanly.

## 7. Pre-shoot measurement checklist

**The run**

- [ ] Write the full weekly task list and fix [TOTAL] before running anything.
- [ ] Write the rubric, with one line per grade and the rule that a pass is three of three.
- [ ] Run each task three times on today's model. Save the prompts, outputs, [MODEL] and date.
- [ ] Count [N], [E] and [F], and confirm they sum to [TOTAL].
- [ ] Run the same prompts on the older model for [OLD_N].
- [ ] Confirm [OLD_MODEL_DATE] from the vendor's own release page, and note the source for the caption.
- [ ] Pick [COMMON_TASK] from the real list: something a CA, teacher or bank RM also does.
- [ ] Pick [TASK]: a three-of-three pass that is really handed over, and preferably one any office worker does.
- [ ] Time [BEFORE_TIME] and [AI_TIME], with the prompt and your check included in [AI_TIME].
- [ ] Pick [FAILED_TASK] and confirm its fail count. Prefer one a non-IT viewer understands in two words.
- [ ] Write down what the freed time actually went to as [FREED_WORK]. Use the real answer, in work terms.
- [ ] Fix [RUN2_DATE] and put it in your calendar.

**The GPT**

- [ ] Build the flow so the viewer types only a job title and 3 tasks (up to 5).
- [ ] Have it write a realistic dummy input for each task itself. A viewer may paste a sanitised one instead.
- [ ] Have it attempt each task once, then ask for a one-word grade: pass, edits or fail.
- [ ] Make the output a dated Run card with:
  - the number;
  - the task to hand over and its prompt;
  - the task that stayed human;
  - a Run #2 date;
  - the label "1 try per task · dummy input · graded by you".
- [ ] Have it refuse employer or client data and offer a dummy input instead.
- [ ] End the card with "Post your number on the Run #1 reel."
- [ ] Test it in a free ChatGPT account with at least three non-IT task sets, and time each run from DM tap to card.
- [ ] Test the DM delivery from a second, non-follower account with the comment "RUN 3", and check where the DM lands.
- [ ] Put the link in your bio.

- **Filled estimate:** 128 to 134 words. This assumes 2 to 4 words each for [TASK], [FREED_WORK] and [FAILED_TASK], and 2 each for the two times and the date.
- **Speech:** 49 to 52 s at 2.6 words per second.
- **Holds:** about 4 s (flip 0.4, look 0.5, screen recording 1.0, tap 0.6, peel 0.8, final card 0.5).
- **Runtime:** about 53 to 56 s, under the 58 s limit. Re-time it once the real values are in.
- **Hook pace:** the eight-word question takes about 2.7 s at 3 words per second, so the flip lands at about 2.8 s. The hook is 10 spoken words and 5 on-screen words.
- **Tight spots:**
  - Scene 4 is 15 words in 6 s with one card press. Rehearse the press on the date.
  - Scene 10 is 29 words in about 12 s with four actions. It is the densest scene.
  - The "AI from a year ago" alternate adds 2 words.
- **If it runs long, cut in this order:**
  1. "from my week" in beat 4.
  2. "Every month," moved to on-screen text in beat 8.
  3. "and your guess" moved to on-screen text in beat 10.

**Two trade-offs to approve**

- **The guess is merged into the RUN comment.** This keeps one action and gives non-runners a way in. It only works if your DM tool matches "RUN" inside a longer comment; test that first.
- **[COMMON_TASK] is circled but not spoken.** Beat 2 already says three non-IT tasks aloud, and saying it too costs about 1 s.
