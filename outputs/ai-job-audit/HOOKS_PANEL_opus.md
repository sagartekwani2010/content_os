## Where I disagree with the writer

- **19v2 should be #1, not #3.** It takes the most stops in the grid and is the most quotable line, which drives sends. The writer listed "undersells the stakes" as a risk, but that is the anti-doom stance the brief asks for. Two risks are real:
  - "AI didn't take my job" can sound smug to someone who was just laid off. Deliver it wryly, not triumphantly.
  - The line only holds if [TASK] is a clean pass that really sat in a Tuesday morning.
- **N1 should drop from #2 to #6.** It explains the format well but doesn't earn a stop. The hook depends on a whiteboard that is unreadable on a phone, and "my whole week" gives the viewer no doorway in.
- **22v2 should rise from #10 to #4.** A report card is a familiar frame for teachers, students and HR. It has a real flaw, though: "[N] pass, [F] fail" drops the "pass with edits" grade, which misrepresents your own published rubric. Fix it to "[N] pass, [E] with edits, [F] fail" or put "Report card: [N] of [TOTAL]" on screen.
- **26v2 has the best on-screen text and the weakest spoken line.** "GST? Lesson plans? Follow-ups?" is the only text that makes the CA, the RM and the teacher stop. "Write down three things…" is a command at 10 pm. Keep the text and change the spoken line to "Here's what AI did to my weekly tasks."
- **28v2 belongs at the bottom.** It is fine as a brand test, but the grid suggests the scale image filters out the non-IT viewers the brief says must see themselves early.

## Problems in the 10-second follow-up (section 5)

Two lines state results that don't exist yet:

- **"This one, it does better than me."** Keep it only if the before/after actually shows that. Otherwise say "This one, it now does."
- **"It failed all three times."** Use it only if the run shows three fails. If it was one or two fails, say so.

Leave both lines as placeholders until Run #1 is done.

## Post first

**Post 19v2 first,** if Run #1 produces a real clean pass on a task you do every week. Change "Tuesday" to whichever day that task actually falls on.

If no task passes cleanly, **post 16v2 instead.** It holds up whatever [N] turns out to be.

Use 22v2, with the rubric fixed, as one of the Trial Reels in place of N2. It covers non-IT viewers better.
