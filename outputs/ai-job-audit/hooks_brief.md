THEME (Reel 1 of a series, English only, Indian audience on Instagram): An Indian AI engineer (visibly fit; known for "Gym Jarvis", AI agents he builds and tests on his own training and food with measure -> act -> re-measure loops) refuses to be a sitting duck while layoffs hit. He tests his own real weekly tasks against today's AI model: each task run 3 times, graded pass / pass with edits / fail on a published rubric, giving a dated count ("Run #1, 18 Nov 2026: 4 of 11 tasks"). He back-tests the same tasks on a model from a year ago to show how fast it moves, hands one task to AI with a real before/after, shows one task AI failed, and promises Run #2 next month: whatever AI can newly do, he offloads, and he moves his time to higher-order work. Series: monthly runs, the task AI failed, where the freed hours went, viewers' jobs. CTA: comment your 3 tasks.
Numbers are placeholders until real: [N] of [TOTAL], [OLD_N], [AI_TIME], [TASK]. Never invent results.
LEARNED: generic layoff/AI-doom openers are saturated in India and lose viewers in 2-3 s; concrete task hooks win; non-IT viewers must see themselves early; "I'm an AI engineer" can read as distance unless paired with vulnerability; no percentages; never insult a profession; never imply laid-off people failed; Instagram rewards watch time and sends.
A hook = the first 1.5-3 s: spoken words (max ~12), on-screen text (max ~6 words), and the first frame.
