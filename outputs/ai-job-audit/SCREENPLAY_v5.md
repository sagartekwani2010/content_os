# SCREENPLAY v5: "My job on trial" (55 s)

## 1. Logline + promise
AI finishes his Monday report while he is still on "Hi team", so he puts his whole job on trial, takes the verdict, and hands the first task over himself.

**Promise:** in 2 minutes you know which tasks to hand to AI today, and which stay yours.

## 4. Screenplay (about 136 spoken words)

**S1 · 0-4.5 s · Cold open**
- **SHOT:** Full-frame split, race already over. AI's finished report on the right, his doc on the left reading "Hi team", one real timer frozen at the AI's finish.
- **SPOKEN:** "Weekly status report. AI: [AI_TIME]. Me? I'm still on 'Hi team'."
- **TEXT:** Weekly status report · AI: [AI_TIME] · ME: "Hi team"
- **DEMO:** The freeze frame from the real one-take race.
- **EMOTION:** Humour.
- **DELIVERY:** Deadpan to lens, hands off the keyboard.
- **CONTINUITY:** Cold in; out sitting back.
- **SFX:** One ding on "[AI_TIME]", then music in.

**S2 · 4.5-11 s · Grid**
- **SHOT:** Standard split, grid on top and him below.
- **SPOKEN:** "Teacher: question paper. Bank RM: follow-up mails. Student: your target job. Your week is in here too."
- **TEXT:** Six chips in three rows of two (1 s per row), then held to the end of the scene, neutral colour, no ratings:

  - CA: GST recon (CA sign-off) · Finance: variance commentary
  - Consulting: first-draft storyline · Marketing: competitor scan

- **DEMO:** The tool's role picker.
- **EMOTION:** Recognition.
- **DELIVERY:** Warm, pointing up at each row as he names it.
- **CONTINUITY:** Sat back; out neutral.
- **SFX:** A soft pop per row.

**S3 · 11-16.5 s · Trial**

- **DELIVERY:** Slower and quieter.

**S4 · 16.5-21.5 s · Verdict**

## 6. Why they run it, why they send it

**Run:**
- It rates their own tasks, where a roadmap gives generic advice.
- Every rating has a reason, and the same input gives the same count.
- The exact prompt is ready to use today.
- It takes 2 minutes, free, no login, and the link is open in the pinned comment.
- It gives counts, never a percentage, and shows what stays human.

**Send:**
- The recipient is anyone worried about AI at work, not only report writers.
- Six senior-safe chips let a viewer tag a CA, RM, teacher or consulting friend.
- The dignity row is safe to forward because it insults no profession.
- The prompt is worth a screenshot.

## 8. Honesty check

| Item | Must be true before posting |
|---|---|
| [AI_TIME], "Hi team" | From one real take. If he was past "Hi team", say and show the real line. |
| The six chips, student toggle | Each exists in the tool with that wording. If not, fix the tool or cut the chip. |
| [MY_N], [MY_TOTAL], [MY_TASK_*] | From his real audit. The S4 take matches his real reaction. |
| "Every rating gives a reason" | Only if the tool shows one per rating. If not, say only "Ran it again. Same count." |
| Run 1 = Run 2 | Two recorded runs, not duplicated footage. |
| [MY_TASK_1], [BEFORE_TIME], [AFTER_TIME], [TIME_SAVED] | The card's real top task (ideally the status report, closing the loop with S1). Times measured, his edit shown. |
| Report and prompt on screen | No company, client or colleague names. |
| [MY_SKILL], [MY_REASON_LINE] | The card's real text. |
| [FRIEND_*] | Written consent, no name or company, a real human row read verbatim. Prefer a CA or FP&A friend. |
| "In two minutes" | Time a real first-time user. Say the measured number. |
| "Link's pinned", comment AUDIT | Link pinned and auto-DM tested before posting. |
| English only | No Hindi in speech, text or tool screens, and no claim of Hindi support. |

**Changed from v4:**
