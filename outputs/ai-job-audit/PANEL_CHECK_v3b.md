**Pattern:** juniors whose task is on a chip watch to the end. Seniors whose chip is missing or reductive (IB, strategy manager, brand manager, RM) swipe before 20 s. Emotion is weakest at 11–23 s, where the screen shows the AI engineer's own tasks rather than the viewer's.

## Checks

**4 or more working hooks:** narrowly yes. Four work:
- "Hi team" at 3 s
- the grid at 5 s
- the sting at 18 s, but only if his number is high
- the dignity row at 34 s

The race at 0 s is unreadable in 3 s. The defiance line at 26 s, "jo card ne bola", sounds obedient rather than defiant.

**Demos real and visible:**
- S4 (ratings in 7 s on a phone recording) and S10 (prompt held 3 s) are readable.
- S7 crams the card, copy, paste, output, an edit and a before/after into 8 s, so none of it reads.
- The race is not real at its stated length.

**Reason to use:** strong. Task-level verdicts plus a ready prompt beat a generic roadmap. "Boss, appraisal, YouTube" adds words and a slight sneer without adding a reason.

**Moments that feel fake, preachy, fearful or insulting:**
- **Fake:** the 3 s "unedited" race.
- **Fake:** "Same input, same count. Yeh guess nahi hai." Consistency is not correctness, and AI-literate viewers (6, 8) will call it out.
- **Preachy:** "Client ka trust AI nahi kama sakta" is a platitude.
- **Insulting:** "Consulting deck formatting" and "HR JD likhna" reduce whole professions to their lowest task.
- **Fear:** "AI kar leta hai" on the teacher's question paper, if the grid implies it.
- **Unclear:** the CTA "jiska Monday report mein jaata hai" is ambiguous on first listen.

## Top 6 fixes

1. **Cut to about 130 words.**
   - S3: drop "Student? Target job chuno" and keep the toggle on screen only. Saves 4 words.
   - S6: "Dobara chalaya, same count." Saves 6.
   - S7: drop "card ka exact prompt". Saves 4.
   - S8: drop the "Client ka trust" line. Saves 7.
   - S9: "Kaunse tasks aapke hi rahenge, koi nahi batata. Yeh batata hai." Saves 7.
   - Then re-time from a recorded read, not estimates.

2. **Fix the race honestly.** Use alternative opening 1 as the main cut: cold open on the finished AI report, his "Hi team", and the real elapsed timer frozen on screen. If you show the race, label the speed-up "4x" and keep the real timer visible.

3. **Rewrite the grid chips at a senior-safe level.**
   - Consulting: "storyline first-draft from notes"
   - IB/ER: "comps refresh / earnings update" (new chip)
   - HR: "JD + first screening shortlist"
   - Bank: "client follow-up mails" (RM work) instead of branch MIS, or label MIS as branch ops
   - Marketing: split into "performance: ad copy variants" and "brand: competitor scan"

   Build the grid, then hold it 1.5 s so it is readable.

4. **Replace the determinism proof with a reason proof.** Show one rating's "kyun" line, for example "rule-based, same template every week". Say: "Har rating ke saath reason." This holds the trust beat without overclaiming what the tool does.

5. **Make S7 legible and properly defiant.**
   - Show only three steps: the prompt, the output, his one-line edit.
   - Put the BEFORE/AFTER numbers large on screen.
   - Change the line to "Toh maine pehla task khud AI ko diya." The "khud" makes it his choice, not the card's.
   - Shorten S4 to 5 s so the viewer's own role returns to screen sooner.

6. **Make the dignity row and CTA specific.**
   - Pick the friend from a high-send role (CA, teacher or FP&A).
   - Read their real human row verbatim, with no moral attached.
   - CTA: "Usko bhejo jiska poora Monday report banane mein jaata hai."
   - Time "2 minute" on a real first-time user before saying it.

## Verdict: **REVISE**

The bones are strong: the "Hi team" laugh, the count instead of a percentage, the dignity row and the screenshot-able prompt.

It cannot ship as written because:
- it runs 7–11 s over 55 s;
- the opening race cannot be both unedited and 3 s long;
- the determinism claim overreaches;
- the grid alienates the senior half of the panel.

Fixes 1–3 are blocking; 4–6 lift run and send. Not checked: the actual tool output, real timings and the audio, which the creator must listen to.
