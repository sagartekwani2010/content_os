# SCREENPLAY v4: "Meri job ka trial" (55 s)

## 1. Logline + promise
AI finishes his Monday report while he is still on "Hi team", so he puts his whole job on trial, takes the verdict, and hands the first task over himself.

**Promise:** in 2 minutes you know which of your tasks to hand to AI today, and which stay yours.

## 4. Screenplay (about 125 spoken words)

**S1 · 0-5 s · Cold open**
- **SHOT:** Full-frame split, race already over. AI's finished report on the right, his doc on the left reading "Hi team", and one real timer frozen at the AI's finish.
- **SPOKEN:** "Weekly status report. AI: [AI_TIME]. Main abhi 'Hi team' pe hoon."
- **TEXT:** Weekly status report · AI: [AI_TIME] · ME: "Hi team"
- **DEMO:** The freeze frame from the real one-take race.
- **EMOTION:** Humour.
- **DELIVERY:** Deadpan to lens, hands off the keyboard.
- **CONTINUITY:** Cold in; out sitting back.
- **SFX:** One ding on "[AI_TIME]", then music in.

**S2 · 5-11 s · Grid**
- **SHOT:** Standard split, grid on top and him below.
- **SPOKEN:** "Isliye poori job audit ki. GST recon, variance commentary, question paper: aapka task bhi yahan hai."
- **TEXT:** Ten chips in five rows of two (0.7 s per row), then a 1.5 s hold, in neutral colour with no ratings:
  - CA: GST recon (CA sign-off) · Teacher: question paper
  - FP&A: variance commentary · IB/ER: comps refresh / earnings update
  - Consulting: storyline first-draft from notes · IT: status report
  - Performance: ad copy variants · Brand: competitor scan
  - HR: JD + first screening shortlist · Bank RM: client follow-up mails
  - The "Student? Target job" toggle, on screen only.
- **DEMO:** The tool's role picker.
- **EMOTION:** Recognition.
- **DELIVERY:** Warm, pointing up at the chips.
- **CONTINUITY:** Sat back; out neutral.
- **SFX:** A soft pop per row.

**S3 · 11-16 s · Trial**
- **SHOT:** Full-frame phone recording.
- **SPOKEN (VO):** "Role: AI engineer. Hafte ke tasks tick kiye. Rating dekho."
- **TEXT:** The tool's own labels.
- **DEMO:** Ticks on [MY_TASK_*], then the three rating types land one by one.
- **EMOTION:** Dread.
- **DELIVERY:** Slower and quieter.
- **CONTINUITY:** VO only.
- **SFX:** Music drops low, one tick per rating.

**S4 · 16-21 s · Verdict**
- **SHOT:** Split, the count on top and his face below.
- **SPOKEN:** Use the take that matches the real number.
  - If high: "AI engineer hoon, phir bhi [MY_N] of [MY_TOTAL]. Bura laga."
  - If low: "Socha tha zyada niklega. [MY_N] of [MY_TOTAL]."
- **TEXT:** [MY_N] of [MY_TOTAL] tasks
- **DEMO:** The count on the result card.
- **EMOTION:** Sting.
- **DELIVERY:** One beat of silence, eyes down, then to lens. No smile.
- **CONTINUITY:** Neutral in; out still.
- **SFX:** Music cuts for 0.5 s.

**S5 · 21-24.5 s · Proof**
- **SHOT:** Split, with one rating's reason line zoomed on top and a small "Run 1 = Run 2" inset.
- **SPOKEN:** "Har rating ke saath reason. Dobara chalaya, same count."
- **TEXT:** Kyun: [MY_REASON_LINE] · Run 1 = Run 2
- **DEMO:** The tool's real reason text and the two identical counts.
- **EMOTION:** Trust.
- **DELIVERY:** Flat and factual, with no claim that it is "correct".
- **CONTINUITY:** Still; out with chin up.
- **SFX:** Double click.

**S6 · 24.5-33 s · Fight-back**
- **SHOT:** Full-frame screen recording in three steps, then large BEFORE/AFTER numbers.
- **SPOKEN:** "Toh maine pehla task khud AI ko diya. [MY_TASK_1]. Pehle [BEFORE_TIME]. Ab [TIME_SAVED] wapas, checking meri."
- **TEXT:** BEFORE [BEFORE_TIME] · AFTER [AFTER_TIME], large
- **DEMO:** Only three things: the card's prompt, the output, his one-line edit.
- **EMOTION:** Defiance.
- **DELIVERY:** Energy up, with a stress on "khud".
- **CONTINUITY:** Chin up; out leaning in.
- **SFX:** Music returns with a beat, plus one whoosh.

**S7 · 33-41 s · Dignity row**
- **SHOT:** Split, the friend's card on top with no name.
- **SPOKEN:** "Mera dost, [FRIEND_ROLE]. [FRIEND_N] of [FRIEND_TOTAL]. Lekin yeh row dekho: [FRIEND_TASK_HUMAN]."
- **TEXT:** AI draft, human sign-off · Insaan chahiye
- **DEMO:** The sign-off flag, then the human row held for 2 s. He reads the row verbatim, with no moral added.
- **EMOTION:** Pride.
- **DELIVERY:** Softer, slower, a small smile, silent during the hold.
- **CONTINUITY:** Leaning in; out open.
- **SFX:** Music warms, with no effects.

**S8 · 41-46 s · Reason**
- **SHOT:** Split, card on top.
- **SPOKEN:** "Kaunse tasks aapke hi rahenge, koi nahi batata. Yeh batata hai. 2 minute."
- **TEXT:** Skill badhao: [MY_SKILL] · 2-week experiment
- **DEMO:** The card scrolls to those two sections.
- **EMOTION:** Power.
- **DELIVERY:** Direct, with no sneer.
- **CONTINUITY:** Open; out pointing at the lens.
- **SFX:** Music builds.

**S9 · 46-55 s · Ask**
- **SHOT:** His prompt full-frame for 3 s with the voice over it, then him full-frame.
- **SPOKEN:** "No login, Hindi mein bhi. Comment AUDIT, job batana optional. Aur usko bhejo jiska poora Monday report banane mein jaata hai."
- **TEXT:** Comment AUDIT · Free · No login · No names, clients or company
- **DEMO:** The exact prompt, readable for a screenshot.
- **EMOTION:** Invitation.
- **DELIVERY:** Smiling and unhurried.
- **CONTINUITY:** Ends on a held look.
- **SFX:** Music resolves, with a final tick.

## 6. Why they run it, why they send it

**Run:**
- It rates their own tasks, where a roadmap gives generic advice.
- Every rating comes with a reason, and the same input gives the same count.
- The exact prompt is ready to use today.
- It takes 2 minutes, with no login, in Hindi, Hinglish or English.
- It gives counts, never a percentage, and shows what stays human.

**Send:**
- The named recipient: "jiska poora Monday report banane mein jaata hai".
- Ten chips at a senior-safe level let a viewer tag a CA, RM or brand friend.
- The dignity row is safe to forward because it insults no profession.
- The prompt is worth a screenshot.

## 8. Honesty check

| Item | Must be true before posting |
|---|---|
| [AI_TIME], "Hi team" | From one real take. If he was past "Hi team", say and show the real line. |
| The ten chips | Each role and task exists in the tool's templates with that wording. If not, add it to the tool or cut the chip. |
| [MY_N], [MY_TOTAL], [MY_TASK_*] | From his real audit. The S4 line matches his real reaction. |
| "Har rating ke saath reason" | Only if the tool shows a reason per rating. If not, say only "Dobara chalaya, same count." |
| Run 1 = Run 2 | Two recorded runs, not duplicated footage. |
| "Pehla task", [BEFORE_TIME], [AFTER_TIME], [TIME_SAVED] | [MY_TASK_1] is the card's real top task (ideally the status report, which closes the loop with S1). Times are measured, and his edit is shown. |
| The report and prompt on screen | No company, client or colleague names. |
| [MY_SKILL] | The card's real text. |
| [FRIEND_*] | Written consent, no name or company, a real human row read verbatim. Prefer a CA, teacher or FP&A friend. |
| "2 minute" | Time a real first-time user. Say the measured number. |
