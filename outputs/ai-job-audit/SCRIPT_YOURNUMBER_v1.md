# Reel 1: "What's your number?" (Run #1, viewer-first version)

I did not open `AGENTS.md`, the `reel-script-writing` skill or `production/REEL_DEFAULTS.md` in this session, so check pace, caption and audio-level rules against them before you approve the beat sheet. Nothing is saved to the repo.

## 1. Title, logline, arc

- **Title:** What's your number? (Run #1)
- **Logline:** An AI engineer asks how many of your weekly tasks AI can do, flips a card to show his own dated number, and hands you a free GPT to get yours.
- **Emotion arc:** curiosity → "he means my job" → "he's exposed too" → trust in a dated number → unease at the one-year gap → "he acted" → curiosity about the taped task → calm → jolt on "For now" → "I want my number".

**Why this version works**

- **The viewer's number is the open loop.** A blank "YOU: ?" card enters at 0:07, sits beside his score all reel, and comes back to the lens in the last frame.
- **The hook is a question about them.** His number is only the answer that proves the question can be answered.
- **The non-IT doorway is spoken.** "GST filing" is said at about 0:05, with the viewer's card on screen.
- **Two loops run at once.** The taped task closes at 0:38 and the viewer's number closes only in the GPT.
- **The props do the drama.** The card flip, the gap left on the board, the tape and the blank card carry it without adjectives.
- **The last frame mirrors the first.** Both are a "?" card at the lens, so a rewatch loops cleanly.

## 2. Screenplay

**Standing setup**

- **Wardrobe:** fitted plain dark tee, no logo.
- **Camera:** phone main lens (1x), vertical 9:16, on a tripod at eye level unless stated.
- **Lighting ("standard"):** one soft key 45° camera-left, with a white bounce on the right. Light the board evenly and check for glare on tape and cards.
- **Layout:** "Full" is full frame. "Top half" puts the visual on top and you speaking to lens below.
- **Sound:** a low pulse bed at the `REEL_DEFAULTS.md` level, with SFX from real sounds recorded on the shoot.
- **Board:**
  - [TOTAL] yellow stickies, one task each, in a column with three hand-drawn grade boxes beside each.
  - A header strip reading "Run #1 · 18 Nov 2026" with an empty card slot under it.
  - A dashed outline labelled "Run #2".
  - The rubric sheet taped on the left.
  - One sticky covered in opaque tape with three red crosses.
- **Laptop:** open on a stool beside the board.

### Scene 1: The flip (0:00-0:04.5)

- **Set:** board soft in the background. You hold an A5 card beside your face with a huge "?" facing the lens.
- **Camera:** full frame, medium close-up, static. Face and card are both sharp in frame one.
- **Lighting:** standard.
- **Action:** hold the "?" through the question, then flip the card on "Mine" to show "[N] of [TOTAL]". Hold for 0.4 s.
- **Spoken:** "How many of your weekly tasks can AI do? Mine: [N]."
- **On-screen text:** How many can AI do? → after the flip: Mine: [N] of [TOTAL]
- **Visual:** the real card, no graphic.
- **Viewer emotion:** curiosity, then "that's a real number".
- **Delivery:** ask it as a real question with eyebrows up. Say "Mine: [N]" flat, with no smile.
- **Continuity:** start still, and end with the card number-side out in your right hand.
- **Sound:** no music under the question. The card snap is up front and the music enters after it.
- **Transition out:** hard cut.

### Scene 2: Your week (0:04.5-0:08.5)

- **Set:** same spot. Your card is lowered, and a second card reading "YOU: ?" is in your left hand, out of frame.
- **Camera:** full frame, punched in about 10%.
- **Lighting:** standard.
- **Action:** raise the "YOU: ?" card to the lens on "Your week".
- **Spoken:** "GST filing? Lesson plans? Follow-ups? Your week has a number too."
- **On-screen text:** GST filing? Lesson plans? Follow-ups? (one phrase at a time, in sync)
- **Visual:** your face, then the real "YOU: ?" card.
- **Viewer emotion:** "he means my job."
- **Delivery:** quick on the three tasks, then slow down and point the card at the lens.
- **Continuity:** end with one card in each hand.
- **Sound:** a soft tick on each task. Music low.
- **Transition out:** cut.

### Scene 3: Not safe either (0:08.5-0:11.5)

- **Set:** same spot, both cards down at your chest.
- **Camera:** full frame, close-up.
- **Lighting:** standard.
- **Action:** none. Stay still.
- **Spoken:** "I build AI. I'm not layoff-proof either."
- **On-screen text:** none (captions only).
- **Visual:** face only.
- **Viewer emotion:** "he's exposed too."
- **Delivery:** level, with a small breath between the sentences. No fear and no bravado.
- **Continuity:** energy drops one notch. The cards stay visible.
- **Sound:** the music thins. No SFX.
- **Transition out:** cut on "So" to the board.

### Scene 4: The method, dated (0:11.5-0:17.5)

- **Set:** the board.
- **Camera:** full frame medium shot for the first 2 s, then top half. The top is a slow pan down the stickies that pauses on three legible rows, and the bottom is you beside the board.
- **Lighting:** standard.
- **Action:**
  1. Press your card into the slot under "Run #1 · 18 Nov 2026".
  2. Stick the "YOU: ?" card beside it.
  3. Count the three grades on your fingers.
- **Spoken:** "So, eighteenth November: [TOTAL] tasks from my real week, three runs each. Pass, edits, or fail."
- **On-screen text:** [TOTAL] tasks · 3 runs · pass / edits / fail (small: [N] passed 3 of 3 · [MODEL] · graded by me · rubric in caption)
- **Visual:** the real board. A hand-drawn circle animates around the [COMMON_TASK] row during the pan.
- **Viewer emotion:** "that's a fair test, and that row is my kind of task."
- **Delivery:** brisk and factual.
- **Continuity:** both cards stay up for the rest of the reel.
- **Sound:** a card slap, then a tick on each grade.
- **Transition out:** cut.

### Scene 5: A year ago (0:17.5-0:21)

- **Set:** board. A smaller card reads "[OLD_MODEL] · [OLD_MODEL_DATE] · [OLD_N] of [TOTAL]".
- **Camera:** top half. The top is a close shot of the old and new cards, and the bottom is your face.
- **Lighting:** standard.
- **Action:** stick the old card to the left of yours and slide your finger from old to new.
- **Spoken:** "AI from a year ago, same tasks: [OLD_N]."
- **On-screen text:** [OLD_N] → [N] (small: same prompts · one comparison)
- **Visual:** the real cards, with a hand-drawn arrow from old to new.
- **Viewer emotion:** quiet unease.
- **Delivery:** even, then a half-second look at the lens. Say nothing more.
- **Continuity:** three cards are now up.
- **Sound:** the music drops to a single note under the look.
- **Transition out:** cut.

### Scene 6: The handover (0:21-0:28)

- **Set:** board and laptop. The [TASK] sticky has three green ticks.
- **Camera:** top half. The top is a 2 s close shot of the sticky move, then about 3 s of screen recording, and the bottom is your face.
- **Lighting:** standard. Check for laptop screen glare.
- **Action:** peel the [TASK] sticky off the board and press it onto the laptop lid. A gap stays in the column.
- **Spoken:** "[TASK] took me [BEFORE_TIME]. Now AI does it, I check it: [AI_TIME]."
- **On-screen text:** [TASK]: [BEFORE_TIME] → [AI_TIME] (small: includes my check)
- **Visual:** the real screen recording, with your old version on the left and the AI output on the right, both scrolling. Employer and client data are removed.
- **Viewer emotion:** "he acted on it."
- **Delivery:** rueful on the first sentence and plain on the second.
- **Continuity:** the gap in the column stays empty until scene 8.
- **Sound:** sticky peel and press. No whoosh.
- **Transition out:** cut to the taped row.

### Scene 7: The taped one (0:28-0:31)

- **Set:** board, the taped sticky with three red crosses.
- **Camera:** full frame, close on the row, with your finger and part of your face in frame.
- **Lighting:** standard.
- **Action:** one tap on the tape, then look at the lens.
- **Spoken:** "This one failed. All three runs."
- **On-screen text:** Failed 3 of 3 · remember this
- **Visual:** the real row.
- **Viewer emotion:** curiosity.
- **Delivery:** lower voice, with a held look.
- **Continuity:** the tape is untouched.
- **Sound:** the music cuts out on the tap and returns on the cut.
- **Transition out:** cut.

### Scene 8: The loop (0:31-0:38)

- **Set:** board, with the gap in the column.
- **Camera:** top half. The top is a close shot of the gap, and the bottom is your face.
- **Lighting:** standard.
- **Action:** write "[FREED_WORK]" on a green sticky and press it into the gap.
- **Spoken:** "Every month, I'll re-run it. What newly passes, I hand over. That time went to [FREED_WORK]."
- **On-screen text:** Measure → hand over → re-measure
- **Visual:** the real board. The three words draw on as a hand-drawn loop, one per sentence.
- **Viewer emotion:** calm. It's a plan, not a pitch.
- **Delivery:** steady and warm. The last sentence is specific and not proud.
- **Continuity:** the green sticky stays in the gap.
- **Sound:** marker and sticky press. The music is at its fullest.
- **Transition out:** cut on the press.

### Scene 9: The reveal (0:38-0:43)

- **Set:** board, the taped row.
- **Camera:** full frame. Start on your face and hand, then end close on the row.
- **Lighting:** standard.
- **Action:** peel the tape in one pull and hold on the name for 0.8 s.
- **Spoken:** "The one it failed? [FAILED_TASK]. Still mine. For now."
- **On-screen text:** [FAILED_TASK] ✗ ✗ ✗
- **Visual:** the real sticky.
- **Viewer emotion:** payoff, then a small jolt.
- **Delivery:** small smile on "Still mine". Drop it on "For now" and hold the look.
- **Continuity:** park the used tape on the board edge.
- **Sound:** the tape rip is up front. No music under "For now."
- **Transition out:** cut on silence.

### Scene 10: Your number (0:43-0:54)

- **Set:** board behind you, with your card, the "YOU: ?" card and the empty Run #2 outline in view.
- **Camera:** full frame, then top half for about 3 s of GPT recording, then full-frame medium close-up to the end.
- **Lighting:** standard.
- **Action:**
  1. Take the "YOU: ?" card off the board on "your number".
  2. Point down on "Comment RUN".
  3. Tap the Run #2 outline on "Run two".
  4. Push the "?" card to the lens on "Bring your number".
- **Spoken:** "Now your number. Comment RUN, I'll DM you my free GPT. It tries your tasks, you grade. Five minutes. Run two, [RUN2_DATE]. Bring your number."
- **On-screen text:** Comment RUN → free GPT · post your number below (small: dummy data only · link in bio)
- **Visual:** the real recording of the Run #1 GPT in a free account. It attempts a dummy non-IT task and the dated Run card appears.
- **Viewer emotion:** "I want my number."
- **Delivery:** friendly and direct. "Bring your number" is a challenge with a smile.
- **Continuity:** the last frame is the "?" card at the lens, in the same position as frame one.
- **Sound:** the music resolves on the card push.
- **Transition out:** hold the "?" for half a second, then end.

## 7. Pre-shoot measurement checklist

- [ ] Run each task three times on today's model. Save the prompts, outputs, [MODEL] and date.
- [ ] Count [N], [E] and [F], and confirm they sum to [TOTAL].
- [ ] Run the same prompts on the older model for [OLD_N], and note [OLD_MODEL] and [OLD_MODEL_DATE].
- [ ] Pick [COMMON_TASK] from the real list: something a CA, teacher or bank RM also does.
- [ ] Pick [TASK]: a three-of-three pass that is really handed over.
- [ ] Time [BEFORE_TIME] and [AI_TIME], with the prompt and your check included in [AI_TIME].
- [ ] Pick [FAILED_TASK] and confirm its fail count.
- [ ] Write down what the freed time actually went to as [FREED_WORK].
- [ ] Fix [RUN2_DATE] and put it in your calendar.

**The GPT**

- [ ] Build the flow: the viewer lists 3 to 5 tasks, it attempts each on dummy or sanitised input, and the viewer grades each one.
- [ ] Make the output a dated Run card with:

- [ ] Have it refuse employer or client data and offer a dummy input instead.
- [ ] End the card with "Post your number on the Run #1 reel."
- [ ] Test it in a free ChatGPT account with at least three non-IT task sets, and time each run.

- **Runtime:** about 53 to 55 s, under the 58 s limit. Re-time it once the real values are in.
