## 1. Title, logline, arc

- **Title:** Run #1: AI vs my own week
- **Logline:** An AI engineer tests every task in his own week against today's AI, shows the dated score, hands one task over, and promises to re-run it every month.
- **Emotion arc:** wry surprise → shared unease → "I could do this too" → respect for the proof → curiosity about the taped task → calm agency → invitation.
