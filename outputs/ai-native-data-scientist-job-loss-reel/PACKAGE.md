## Brief fingerprint

- **Topic:** AI and the Data Scientist role — why model-output work is becoming cheaper and what evidence now differentiates candidates
- **Core thesis:** AI is not making Data Science irrelevant, but it is reducing the scarcity of notebooks, baseline models and polished analytical outputs. Data Scientists should differentiate themselves by proving ownership of the complete decision system: decision definition, data validity, decision-aware evaluation, deployment, monitoring and bounded response to failure.
- **Learning objective:** After viewing, a Data Scientist can redesign one portfolio case study so it demonstrates a business decision, a data contract, decision-aware evaluation and a production feedback loop rather than ending with model accuracy.
- **Format:** Reel
- **Brand pillar:** ai_career

## Superseded direction — prior evidence-led cut

## Why this version should perform better

1. **The opening contains a real contradiction.** Overall announced cuts are
   down while technology cuts are sharply up; viewers need an explanation.
2. **The personal Amazon observation earns the transition.** It explains why
   Sagar himself once viewed scientist roles as comparatively insulated.
3. **Foundation-model convergence is the mechanism.** The Reel explains why
   routine specialist model production has become cheaper instead of blaming
   an undefined force called AI.
4. **The preparation advice follows the mechanism.** If model production is
   cheaper, candidates must prove decision, data, evaluation and operating
   ownership.
5. **The visuals are evidence, not decoration.** Static source and explanation
   cards should reinforce conversion without displacing the human speaker.

## Hook options

1. **Selected — numerical contradiction:** `Overall layoffs are down 39%. Technology layoffs are up 54%.`
2. **Personal reversal:** `The first layoffs I saw spared scientists. That protection is disappearing.`
3. **Specialisation collapse:** `AI did not kill NLP or Computer Vision. It killed the need for another narrow model.`
4. **Scarcity shift:** `Models were scarce. Now judgement and accountability are.`

Option 1 opens a question the rest of the Reel can answer and uses verified
numbers without claiming AI caused every cut.

## Evidence and interpretation boundary

- Challenger's figures are announced cuts and employer-cited reasons. They do
  not establish that every announcement became a termination or that AI was the
  measured causal mechanism.
- The BLS figure is a ten-year U.S. occupation projection, not evidence that
  current hiring is easy or that an individual job is protected.
- Sagar's Amazon statement is a scoped first-hand observation. It must not be
  expanded into a claim about every Amazon team or layoff wave.
- Salesforce reporting names data analytics and Agentforce among affected
  teams. Oracle's filing explicitly connects AI adoption to workforce
  reductions but does not isolate Data Scientist roles.
- Dell should not be used as Data Scientist-specific proof without a verified
  role breakdown. It may appear only as broad AI-era restructuring context.
- Foundation-model homogenisation is a supported technical mechanism; claiming
  it caused any individual layoff remains a practitioner hypothesis.
- NLP and Computer Vision remain active fields. The narrower claim is that a
  reusable multimodal model can replace many routine task-specific training
  pipelines.
