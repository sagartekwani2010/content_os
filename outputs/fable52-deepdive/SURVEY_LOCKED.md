## Does each reel evoke its intended emotion?

- **Main (positive): partly.** The hook lands as a cost shock ("I'm overpaying"), which is surprise mixed with loss aversion, not warmth. Positive feeling arrives at the Fermat line for builders and at the router card for the fin group. Mass viewers feel nothing positive because they never pay per task.
- **Trial (fear): yes for about 30 s, then it deflates.** The 43% and 63% findings read as "creepy" and "lazy" rather than frightening. The cut to a rupee bill and food grams breaks the arc. The relief at the end is thin because the hook's question is never answered, which you already noted. Personas 8 and 10 read it as bait.

## Where each reel loses people

**Main**
- **0–3 s, mass group:** "Fable 5.2 vs Haiku 5.5" and per-task rupees mean nothing to people on free ChatGPT.
- **~15–30 s:** "Orchestrator" and Fermat are abstract for non-tech viewers.
- **~39 s:** the food test with no personal stake.
- **~65 s:** "Paying for Claude?" tells non-payers this part isn't for them.

**Trial**
- **~32–39 s, the MY TESTS card:** the biggest drop. Viewers came for humanity-risk and get a bill.
- **~39 s:** the grams test, mass group.
- **Bookend:** the fin and consult viewers feel the hook question was a trick.

## Accuracy and fear-mongering vs FACTS

**Main**

1. **"264 page report"** is not in FACTS. FACTS cites page 226, so the card is at least that long, but 264 itself needs checking. The same number appears in both reels.
2. **"A thousand Fable agents produced a proof of Fermat's Last Theorem."** Spoken, this sounds like new mathematics. FACTS says it was a Lean formalization, on an earlier snapshot, built on an earlier formalization. The caveats are only on screen.
3. **"Like a manager, Fable plans and cheaper models do the work."** Orchestrator is an optional role, but the line states it as how the model always works. It should say "can".
4. **"Hard judgement: Fable was closer."** This generalises past FACTS: the gap against Opus is not conclusive. The screen covers it, but the spoken line does not.
5. **"Everyone around me is building with it"** is unverifiable. **"Same scores… twenty tasks"** is slightly off because only 19 were scored.

**Trial**

1. **Spoken hook drops "if managed poorly".** Audio-only and fast viewers hear an unconditional claim. This is the main fear-mongering risk, and the on-screen text only partly mitigates it.
2. **The S-1 quote is shown as alarm without saying it comes from the filing's risk-factor section.** That framing amplifies fear.
3. **43% eval awareness is shown with no context.** It suggests deception, which the card does not claim.
4. **The bookend equivocates.** Amodei's "managed poorly" is about society and governance, but the reel reframes it as "check its work". Persona 14 would call this out.
5. **Beat 6 (63%) is accurate,** but placed next to beat 5 it implies general laziness.

## Top 5 fixes

1. **Trial hook:** speak the condition. For example: "Its CEO says AI, managed poorly, could risk humanity. Seven weeks later, he launched this." Then drop the unanswerable "why", or answer it from FACTS: "his company's answer is safeguards, and here's what its own report found." Rewrite the bookend so it doesn't borrow his phrase for a different meaning.
2. **Trial pivot at ~32 s:** add a spoken bridge into MY TESTS, such as "So is it worth the risk, and the price? I tested it." Label the S-1 line as risk-factor language, and add a 3-word context line on the 43% ("an audit stat").
3. **Main accuracy:** say "can plan" for the orchestrator line, and "finished a machine-checked proof… building on earlier work" for Fermat. Add an on-screen gloss: "a famous centuries-old maths problem" (source it before use). Verify 264 pages and drop "everyone around me".
4. **Main first 3 s for the mass group:** keep the model names on screen only. Say "Claude's newest AI against its cheapest". Frame the lesson universally, "the priciest AI isn't smarter at everyday work", rather than per-task rupees.
5. **Pro beat in both reels:** "Paying for Claude?" excludes most Indian viewers. Lead with the router rule, which applies to everyone, and keep Pro tips as the second line. Check that runtime margin still holds after the change.
