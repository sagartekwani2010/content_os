Fable 5.2 deep-dive TRIAL (CEO question hook, fear; cold palette, red accents; trial beats differ in opening and paper section): mood board

Concept

A cold case file that thaws: the CEO's own warning opens on a near-black, desaturated frame with red marks, the paper's evidence is pinned onto one growing hand-drawn pipeline, and from 38 s an amber accent takes over as control moves into the viewer's hands ("So manage it well").

Palette

#0A0E14 background: cold blue-black, every panel and card
#E6EDF3 chalk white: solid lines = disclosed facts, main lettering
#7B8794 slate grey: dashed lines = my read, ghosted steps, legends, footnotes
#5CC8FF ice cyan: pipeline structure, probes, model lanes, timeline
#FF3B3B red accent: fear only - warning marks, highlights on page cuts, 43%, 1 in 4, '7 WEEKS', Haiku's 410 g miss
#FFC857 amber: relief and control, first seen at 38 s - RELEASED, wrapper gate, ticks, 34% lower, 97% lower, 'So manage it well.'
#F2EEE3 paper off-white: page cut-outs and receipts (desaturated, never pure white)

Type

Section cards: huge condensed uppercase grotesque, chalk white on #0A0E14, one red or amber keyword, tight tracking. Diagram labels: hand-lettered marker caps, 2 weights only (big concept, small detail). Numbers: condensed bold, at least 3x label size. Page cuts: the document's own serif, untouched, with a hand-drawn highlighter stroke. Config recording: the editor's monospace, zoomed. Captions: one low line, white with dark stroke, above Instagram's bottom overlay; active word in red until 38 s, amber after.

Texture

Slightly wobbly marker line (about 7 px at 1440 wide, round caps) that draws on left to right; dashed for 'my read'. Fine film grain and a soft vignette on the dark background. No glow in the fear half except a faint red bloom on highlights; from 38 s amber lines get a soft warm glow. Page cut-outs: off-white, scissor-cut edges, 2 degree tilt, soft shadow, as if taped onto the board. Creator grade: saturation down about 25% with a cool tint until 38 s, easing to neutral by 48 s; never fully warm.

Music

Original low drone with sparse sub pulses and no melody from 0 to 38 s; darker bed under the Instagram montage; full silence for the 1 s quote hold at 28.5 s. From 38 s the drone lifts into the main reel's lighter pulse, with a beat drop on the wrapper hold at 46.8 s. Steady, understated bed through the systems and tips. Drone returns for about 1 s at 84.6 s under the quote callback, then a riser that cuts to hard silence on the final clip.

Pacing

Hook 0-8.5 s: three frame types in 8.5 s (split, punch-in, full-frame number) so it never sits still. Montage 8.5-13.5 s: three hard cuts. Paper section 13.5-48 s: one diagram that keeps growing, broken by a full-frame page cut with silence (27.2 s), a full-frame eye (29.5 s), a full-creator release (38 s) and a full-frame pull-back (46.8 s); a new visual every 2-3.5 s. Systems 48-72 s: calmer, 3-4 s per frame, with one real plate and one real screen recording full-frame. Tips 72-81.5 s: one card, four ticks on the words. Payoff and CTA 81.5-89 s: quick, three cuts. All times are the screenplay's estimates; cue every visual to the spoken word, landing on or a few frames before it.

References

Investigative documentary evidence boards: cut-out document passages taped to a dark board with one highlighter stroke each
Blueprint and architecture line drawings: thin bright lines on near-black, solid versus dashed to separate known from inferred
Cold thriller title sequences: desaturated blue-black frames with a single red accent and long holds
Chalkboard explainer videos: one diagram that keeps growing instead of slides
Hospital-monitor style readouts for the reasoning-monitor strips: calm scan lines, one going flat
Printed till receipts drawn as line art for the cost comparison

Risks

The pasted screenplay stops mid-sentence in the trial-differences note; these keyframes follow the full T1-T15 table, so anything that came after that point is not reflected.
All times are the screenplay's estimates. Beat 12 (61-72 s) has about 4 s more than its words need; the models.yaml recording fills it, but trim there first if the reel runs long.
The CEO quote must be verbatim with its attribution line on screen. Text only: no portrait, no UN or company logo, no fake news-headline styling.
KF12-KF14 have no page cuts yet [PAGE CUT NEEDED]. Do not mock up a page or add a page number; run them as drawings until the real passages are located.
The 'Only when instructed to' and 'same model, measured on Mythos 5.2' tags must stay readable for the full beat, or the 43% and 1-in-4 claims read as stronger than the script states.
The eye-in-the-diamond can tip into sci-fi horror. Keep it a plain line icon, no glow, no glitch effects beyond the 6-frame flicker.
D7 bars are [DUMMY]. Show the label on every bar and the winner, and no digits, until real results exist.
Seven beats reuse main-reel takes recorded in a positive register. Check energy, grade and framing against the fresh trial takes, especially the cut from KF03 into the montage line and from KF09 into KF10.
The receipts (D1) are never set up in the trial hook, so KF30 must read on its own in 3 s: both model labels, both prices, one big number.
KF06 packs the frame, legend, S1, S2 and a page cut into under 2 s. Keep S1/S2 small and grey, and let the chain finish under 'And here's the strange part'.
The KF22 plate must be the real weighed plate with a legible 340 g display; a restaged or stock plate would undermine the only physical proof in the reel.
The full Saturday tease is 13 words and cannot be read in 1 s. KF33 uses a 3-word version; put the full line in the caption if it must appear.
Red is for fear only and amber only from 38 s. Mixing them earlier flattens the turn at KF15.
Instagram clips need permission and visible handles; without them, KF04 has nothing to show.
