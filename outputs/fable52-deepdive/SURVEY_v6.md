## Inaccuracies and risks vs FACTS

I found no outright factual errors. These are the wording risks:

1. **"Billed twenty-two rupees"** (both reels): ₹22 is list price × tokens, not an actual invoice. Say "cost".
2. **"Almost forty"**: the real ratio is 36.7x. This is defensible, but a sceptic (persona 8 or 14) will check it. "Nearly 37 times" is airtight.

6. **Hook A's "just"**: the word itself is accurate (the launch was 10 days ago), but it still implies the quote came near the launch. The on-screen date covers this.
