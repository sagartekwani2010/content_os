## 2. Screenplay

The set, camera and lighting are my proposal; check them against `RECORDING_KIT.md` and `REEL_DEFAULTS.md`. These constants hold for every scene unless noted, and on-screen text is exactly as in the script table.

- **SET:** Desk, plain dark wall, no visible screens or logos. Props are a closed laptop at frame edge and a printed page stack, used in beat 3 only. Wardrobe is a plain dark, matte, logo-free top.
- **CAMERA:** One locked 9:16 shot at eye level, chest-up, eyes on the lens. The layout is split: diagram in the top half, face in the bottom half. Frame the face so the bottom-half crop keeps the eyes in its upper third, and leave clean headroom for a 10% punch-in.

- **Diagram style:** Hand-drawn white line on a dark ground. Amber marks findings, red marks the bill and the "?", white marks the final tick.

**Record-order notes**

- **Block A (tense), beats 1–8:** record first, in order, so the low register stays consistent. Speak at your natural 2.6 words/s; the 1.1x speed-up happens in the edit.
- **Block B (warming), beats 9–13:** take a 30 s reset, then record in order. The warmth should arrive on beat 9, not before.
- **Block C (close), beats 14–16:** record beat 14 twice as a set, since it starts in the Block A register and ends in Block B's.
- **Hook again at the end:** re-record beat 1 three more times once you are warmed up, and keep the stare for 1 s after "this" on every take.
- **Takes:** three clean, complete takes per beat. If a line breaks, restart the whole beat; there are no mid-sentence splices.
- **Holds:** on beats 8 and 13, stay frozen for 2 s after the last word so the 1 s hold has footage.
- **Sync and room tone:** clap at the start of each block and record 30 s of room tone at the end.
