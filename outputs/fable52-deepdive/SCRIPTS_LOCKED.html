    • TONE GUIDE

    Main reel (positive). Deliver it like good news to a friend: smile in the voice, upward inflection on the numbers, a small laugh on "about one on the cheapest", and real awe on the Fermat line. Use bright, mid-tempo music in a major key that never drops out except for the plate hold, with light sound effects (coin dings, chimes, pops). I'd use a warm, light palette with green for savings and ticks, and gold for the Fermat grid; check that against REEL_DEFAULTS.md. Motion is springy, with bars that bounce on landing and cards that slide in. The last image is the green "you" tick.

    Trial reel (fear). Deliver it lower and slower in the first 30 s, flat and serious, with a beat of silence after the hook question and after "being tested"; do not act scared, just state it. From beat 9, let the voice warm to the same calm as the main reel, so the arc is tension then relief. Music is a sparse minor drone with sub hits, which gives way to a calm pad at the food result and returns only briefly at the bookend. I'd use a dark background with amber and red for the findings and the burning bill, and motion that is slow and linear. Keep the tables, the router card and the Pro card pixel-identical in both reels, so any difference in saves and comments comes from hook and tone, not from the data design.
