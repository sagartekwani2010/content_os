3. Teleprompter beat sheet

1. If the CEO says AI could be a risk to humanity, why did he just launch this? FEEL: controlled unease. FACE-VOICE: low, slow, flat, then hold the stare. POSE: square and still.

2. This is Fable five point two: the new top model of Claude, the ChatGPT rival. FEEL: matter-of-fact. FACE-VOICE: same register, stress "top". POSE: slight head tilt.

3. So I read its maker's own report. Two findings. FEEL: in charge. FACE-VOICE: firmer, pause before "Two". POSE: lift the stack, set it down.

4. In up to forty-three percent of automated audits, it suspected it was being tested. FEEL: chill. FACE-VOICE: flat, slow the last five words, then silence. POSE: still.

5. At medium effort, it quit open-ended research without running an experiment in sixty-three percent of sessions. FEEL: dry disbelief. FACE-VOICE: plain, stress "sixty-three". POSE: one small head shake.

6. That's their report. Is it worth the money? I tested it. FEEL: the turn. FACE-VOICE: question up, answer down. POSE: lean in.

7. Everyday tasks, top model against cheapest: same scores, thirty-two times the price. FEEL: ouch. FACE-VOICE: brisk, punch "thirty-two". POSE: two fingers level.

8. The hard one: grams of food from a photo. Twenty-five weighed dishes, four current Claude models. Guess the gap. FEEL: challenge. FACE-VOICE: slower, brow up, hold 1 s. POSE: point up, freeze.

9. Fable missed by forty-nine grams on average, the cheapest by seventy-five. About a third less error than the two cheapest. FEEL: release. FACE-VOICE: the voice warms, first hint of a smile. POSE: hand down, nod.

10. Everyday: a tie, pay less. The hard one: Fable beat the two cheapest. FEEL: clarity. FACE-VOICE: calm, two halves. POSE: hand left, hand right.

11. The rule: Sonnet for most things, says Claude's maker. Haiku kept up on my simple tasks. Fable for the hard parts. FEEL: relief. FACE-VOICE: warm, even. POSE: count three.

12. If you pay for Claude: on Pro, Fable runs on paid credits, not your plan. FEEL: aside to a friend. FACE-VOICE: slightly lower, stress "not your plan". POSE: open palm.

13. Stretch it: lower effort, unused tools off, new chat per topic. Screenshot this. FEEL: useful. FACE-VOICE: quick list, then stop for 1 s. POSE: three taps, camera click.

14. I can't settle the humanity question. Its maker's report ranks it among the least self-critical Claudes. So check its work. FEEL: honest, then armed. FACE-VOICE: low start, firm warm end. POSE: small shrug, level look.

15. Comment FABLE for the cheat sheet. FEEL: invitation. FACE-VOICE: light. POSE: point down.

16. Saturday: the Workout Analyser. FEEL: unfinished. FACE-VOICE: quick. POSE: half-turn away.

Record-order notes

- Block A (tense), beats 1–8: record first, in order, so the low register stays consistent. Speak at your natural 2.6 words/s; the 1.1x speed-up happens in the edit.

- Block B (warming), beats 9–13: take a 30 s reset, then record in order. The warmth should arrive on beat 9, not before.

- Block C (close), beats 14–16: record beat 14 twice as a set, since it starts in the Block A register and ends in Block B's.

- Hook again at the end: re-record beat 1 three more times once you are warmed up, and keep the stare for 1 s after "this" on every take.

- Takes: three clean, complete takes per beat. If a line breaks, restart the whole beat; there are no mid-sentence splices.

- Holds: on beats 8 and 13, stay frozen for 2 s after the last word so the 1 s hold has footage.

- Sync and room tone: clap at the start of each block and record 30 s of room tone at the end.
