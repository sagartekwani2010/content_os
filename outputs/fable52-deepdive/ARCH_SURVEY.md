## The architecture section

**Clarity.** It is too dense for the mass group. MAIN packs five ideas into about 18 s (M3–M7), one every 4.5 s. The builders follow it. Personas 3, 4, 5, 12 and 15 drop out between M4 and M6.

**Build-up.** The "?" box gaining a layer per beat is the best visual idea in the reel. The problem is that the box has no running label, so a casual viewer can't tell what is accumulating. Persona 14 also objects that two-configs, fallback and effort are deployment facts, not architecture. "What they do admit" is the more honest framing.

**What lands:**
- **"One model, shipped two ways" (M4):** a clean reveal for builders and finance.
- **"Effort dial sets the bill" (M6):** it ties back to the ₹ hook, so everyone gets it.
- **"Least self-critical… remember that" (M7 → M11):** the strongest setup and payoff in the reel.
- **In TRIAL, 43% and "one time in four" (T5/T6):** concrete numbers that even the mass viewers feel.

**What loses people:**
- **The fallback beat (M5):** jargon, and nothing pays it off later in MAIN.
- **"You only see a summary":** there is no stake for a casual viewer.
- **The silent training arrow:** invisible at this speed, which is fine as a texture but carries no meaning.
- **"Lean proof" in the M2 on-screen text:** reads as noise.

## Claims not supported by FACTS / ARCH FACTS

1. **M2 "People are doing wild things with it":** this was Anthropic's own test on an earlier snapshot, not users. The spoken line overclaims; the caveat is only on screen.
2. **T8 "…and put classifiers in front":** the classifiers target misuse (cyber, bio, extracting its reasoning and so on), not eval-awareness, sandbagging or reward hacking. Placed right after T5–T7, the line implies a fix that the card doesn't describe. **This is the most serious accuracy problem.**
3. **M12 "Fable led by [XX]%":** asserts a result that hasn't been measured. Even with a placeholder number, the direction itself is invented.
4. **M5 "blocked requests fall back":** spoken as universal. The facts say it happens in Anthropic's apps only and is opt-in on the API.
5. **M9 "it reasons from the plate and spoon":** the facts support only "the pipeline asks it to name the cues it used." That is not proof of how it reasons.
6. **M16 vs M8/M9, a story contradiction:** the payoff says "Haiku matched Fable" right after Haiku was worst on the plate test. Both claims are true, but the viewer hears a contradiction.
7. **The brief isn't met:** the creator asked for 3 ranked Saturday tease options and an open loop. "Saturday: the Workout Analyser" is only a label.

"Nearly forty times" (36.7×), "97% lower", "≈21×", "7 weeks" (47 days) and the quote all check out.

## Top 6 fixes

1. **Fix T8:** say "But Anthropic measured it with probes and published every number." Drop the classifier claim there, or say what the classifiers actually block.
2. **Resolve the Haiku contradiction:** change M16 to "on those eleven *text* jobs, Haiku matched Fable…". That turns the plate test into the lesson: cheap for words, Fable for eyes. Persona 8 and the builders will reward it.
3. **Thin MAIN's architecture block:** cut M5 (saves about 4.5 s), keep the fallback in TRIAL only, and add a running label on the box ("what they admit: 1/4…"). Open the block with "Here's where your ₹22 goes" so the mass group has a reason to stay.
4. **Write a real Saturday tease** in the time freed by fix 3. Ranked options:
   - (a) A frame of the analyser drawing on his squat: "Saturday, I gave Fable my squat. What it saw, I didn't expect."
   - (b) "Fable saw my plate. Saturday: can it see my form?"
   - (c) For TRIAL: "Saturday, I trusted it with my spine."

   Option (a) uses "didn't expect", which only works if the creator really didn't expect the result. Otherwise use (b).
5. **M12:** cut it, or rewrite it after tomorrow's data as "On my judgement tests, [winner] led by X%". Don't record a direction before the numbers exist.
6. **Accuracy wording sweep:**
   - M2 → "Anthropic ran a thousand Fable agents…"
   - M5 → "in Claude's apps"
   - M9 → "it's asked to name the cues it used: plate, spoon."
