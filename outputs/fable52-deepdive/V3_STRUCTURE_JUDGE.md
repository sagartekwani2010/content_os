## 3. Changes, ranked

1. **Replace beat 2 with a tease of the 63% number.** This removes the first swipe point and holds a loop through the sagging list until beat 7 pays it.
2. **Reorder the list to 4, 6, 5, 7 and reword 5 analogy-first.** The two exam analogies chain together, and 5 becomes survivable just before the payoff.
3. **Reword beat 8: open with a question, caveat in the middle, end on the number.** It stays just as honest but no longer ends on a shrug.
4. **Replace beat 9 with the CEO payoff.** This closes the beat 1 loop and sets up beat 11. "How I use Fable" can go on screen as a caption.

Do not move the plate test earlier. It is hedged, so it cannot carry the open, whereas 63% can.
