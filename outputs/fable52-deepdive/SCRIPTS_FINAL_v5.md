# EXPERIMENT DESIGN

**Hypotheses**

- **A (warning hook):** a named CEO, the UN and "risk to humanity" stop more non-tech viewers in the first 3 s than rupees do, and the fear-then-calm arc earns more sends. The risk is that viewers who came for the warning leave when the content turns to cost, so saves and FABLE comments per reach are lower.
- **B (money hook):** the opening promises exactly what the reel delivers, so fewer viewers stop but those who stay save and comment at a higher rate. The earlier panel predicted this pattern for a money hook against a wonder hook.

**What A vs B tells you**

- Whether a broad news hook or a payload-matched hook is the better default for a deep-dive reel.
- Whether a hook that widens the top of the funnel still converts to saves and comments, or only adds views.

**Metrics and what each decides**

| Metric | Decides | Read it as |
|---|---|---|
| 3-s hold | Which hook stops the scroll | Percentage of views that pass 3 s |
| Average watch time | Whether the hook's promise matches the body | Percentage of length and seconds, because A is 11 s longer |
| Sends per reach | Whether the story is worth forwarding | Sends per 1,000 accounts reached |
| Saves per reach | Whether the cards landed | Saves per 1,000 accounts reached |
| FABLE comments per reach | Whether the CTA worked | Comments containing FABLE per 1,000 accounts reached |

Also compare the retention curves at two matched points: the start of the food test and the Pro card. That removes the cold-open length difference.

**How to run it on Instagram**

1. Post A as the main reel and B as a Trial Reel to non-followers.
2. Use the same caption, cover, audio mix, hashtags and FABLE keyword on both.
3. Post both inside the same day window, ideally within an hour of each other.
4. Do not boost, cross-post to Stories or pin a comment on one and not the other.
5. Read the results at 24 h and again at 48 h; decide on the 48 h numbers.
6. Compare rates per reach, never raw counts.

**One confound to handle:** the main reel reaches followers and non-followers, while the Trial Reel reaches non-followers only. Where Insights splits A's views by follower status, use A's non-follower figures against B. Where it does not, treat any A lead on saves and comments with caution, since followers save and comment more. Posting both as Trial Reels and sharing the winner afterwards would be a cleaner test, if you are open to changing the setup.

**Decision rule for future reels** (the thresholds are my suggestion; adjust them)

- **Read nothing below about 1,000 views per version.** Under that, the result is "no read".
- **A's hook wins** if its 3-s hold is at least 5 points higher and its saves plus FABLE comments per 1,000 reach are no more than 20% below B's. Future deep dives then open with the news or stakes hook.
- **B's hook wins** if its saves plus FABLE comments per 1,000 reach are at least 20% higher and its 3-s hold is within 5 points of A's. Future deep dives then open with the result.
- **Split result** (A wins hold and sends, B wins saves and comments): choose by the reel's goal. Use the stakes hook for reach reels and the result hook for reels built around a card or a comment CTA.
- **One pair is one data point.** Lock a default only after the same direction shows up on three reels.
