**Record-order notes**

1. **Start:** record 30 s of room tone, then one full read-through as a warm-up that also serves as a pacing reference.
2. **Section order:** record section 2 (beats 6–9) first, then section 3 (10–12), then section 1 (3–5), then 13–15. Record the hook (beats 1–2) last, when your energy is highest.
3. **Takes:** three clean takes per beat. Each take is the whole beat from the first word to the last, because there are no mid-sentence splices. If you stumble, restart the beat.
4. **Beat 5:** record it as two complete sentences with a clear breath between them, so the cut falls on the sentence boundary. It is the longest line; expect extra takes.
5. **Holds:** on beats 7 and 12, freeze for two full seconds after the last word so the 1 s hold has clean footage.
6. **Pose matching:** start each beat in the "out" pose of the previous one. Beats 1 and 2 must match each other because they sit together.
7. **Beat 3 pickup:** also record "It's called Fable five point two. I read its maker's two hundred sixty-four page report." Use it only if you verify 264; it adds about 1 s and brings the total to about 87.0 s.
8. **Beat 8 pickup:** if FACTS shows the Haiku figure rounds to 74, also record the line with "seventy-four".
