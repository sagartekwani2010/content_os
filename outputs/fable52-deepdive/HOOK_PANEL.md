## Accuracy flags

**Must fix**
- **M1, M5, M7: "forty" / "40x".** ₹22 ÷ ₹0.60 = 36.7x. Say "nearly forty" and show "~37x" or "Nearly 40x". The locked change note says "Forty times the price", and that line itself needs the same fix.
- **T10: "The AI is on my laptop."** Fable runs in Anthropic's cloud, not locally, so this overclaims. The S-1 (26 Oct) also predates the 9 Nov launch and talks about Anthropic's technology in general, not Fable 5.2. Also check that 26 Oct is the filing date and not just the date of the Fortune report.

**Watch**
- **M4: "only one kind of job".** In the routing file, Fable does scripts, hooks, story judging, visual planning and edit decisions. "One kind" (judgement) can stand, but the first frame highlights a single row, which misrepresents the file. Highlight all of Fable's rows.
- **M5 and M9: "until this plate" / "earned its price elsewhere".** These claims need the right comparison:
  - The plate only supports Fable vs Haiku (portion error 34% lower). Fable vs Opus is not conclusive.
  - M9's "elsewhere" depends on tomorrow's reasoning results, which are still [DUMMY]. Don't lock M9 until they come in.
- **"Same scores".** It holds for Fable vs Haiku on all 11 tasks, and M1, M2, M6 and M8 are fine on that basis. Keep the comparison Fable vs Haiku. Sonnet beat Fable on writing (17 vs 16), so never say "the priciest scored best".
- **T1 and T6 drop "if managed poorly" from the audio.** For T1, the on-screen quote handles it. T6 has no quote on screen, so it misquotes by omission.
- **T7 and T9: which configuration was measured.** These figures were measured on Mythos 5.2. It is the same model, so the claim holds, but cite it as "maker's system card".
- **T9 drops "open-ended" from the audio.**
- **Timing.** No hook says the warning came after the launch. "Says" in T1 is present tense, so show the 23 Sep date on screen so nobody reads it as a post-launch remark.
- **No "wipe out" wording and no p(doom) figure** appear anywhere. Good.
