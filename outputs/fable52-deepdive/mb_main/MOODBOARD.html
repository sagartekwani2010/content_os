Fable 5.2 deep-dive MAIN (money hook, positive): mood board

Concept

Receipt to blueprint to receipt: two hand-drawn receipts ask "am I overpaying?", one warm chalk pipeline grows step by step with real paper cut-outs, the creator's own two systems show where the price is earned, and the receipts return to pay off the hook.

Palette

#14110F background: warm near-black for every panel and card
#FFF3DC chalk: main line work, labels, caption text
#FFD23F accent: numbers, the rupee sign, highlighter sweeps on paper cuts, MY READ tags
#FF8A3D Fable 5.2: its receipt, tag, lane and the lit vision slot
#6EC5FF Haiku 5.5: its receipt, tag and lane
#FF7EB6 Opus 5.5: lane and tag (kept neutral in the plate frame)
#C4A7FF Sonnet 5.5: lane only
#7CE38B positive: ticks, RELEASED, 97% lower cost, the shrinking error bar
#FF6B5E caution, used small: the warning mark in M5 and Haiku's overshoot only, never as a wash
#8C857A warm grey: dashed 'my read' lines, NOT DISCLOSED chips, ghosted S4/S5, footnotes
#F6EBD2 paper cream: real page crops with a torn edge

Type

Section cards: huge condensed uppercase sans (Anton or Bebas style), chalk white with one word or number in yellow, filling about 80% of frame width. Diagram labels: hand-lettered marker face, upper-case for node names, minimum 44 px at 1440 wide; footnotes minimum 32 px in warm grey. Numbers: condensed bold, 2 to 3 times the label size, yellow; check the face has a rupee glyph. Paper cuts keep the PDF's own serif, never re-typed. Captions: one line, bold clean sans, chalk white with a dark stroke, sitting low in the creator half but above Instagram's bottom overlay (check against the safe-zone guide), max about 5 words per caption.

Texture

4 to 6 px chalk-marker lines with slight wobble and round caps, drawn on stroke by stroke. Solid line = disclosed, dashed warm-grey line = my read, used consistently from the legend onward. Warm near-black background with faint paper grain, no gradients, no 3D, no stock icons. Soft 8 to 12 px glow on accent strokes only. Paper cuts: cream page crops, scissor/torn edge, 2 degree tilt, soft shadow, yellow highlighter sweep at about 60% opacity. Creator footage: warm grade, lifted skin tones, 3 to 5% grain, applied in post so the same takes can be graded cold for the trial reel.

Music

Upbeat, warm bed around 100 to 110 BPM: plucks or kalimba, soft kick, light bass, starting on the first receipt tick. Bass drops out and the bed thins on "the strange part" (curious, not dark), rebuilds through graded RL, one-second music-only breath for the effort dial, beat drop on the "wrapper" hold, brighter lift into the Meal Analyser section, bass accent on "It thinks", steady under the tips, receipt motif returns at the payoff, riser on the CTA that cuts to silence on the glimpse. Licensed or royalty-free, ducked about 18 dB under voice. No drone anywhere in this cut; that belongs to the trial reel.

Pacing

About 90 to 91 s, a visual change roughly every 3 s. Fastest at the hook (three changes in 6.5 s) and the montage (about 1.6 s per clip); the paper section runs one step per 4.5 to 7 s as draw first, paper cut second; longest holds are the plate numbers (about 4 s) and the wrapper pull-back (about 2.5 s). Timing note: beats M1 to M5 follow the screenplay clock; from 29 s I re-estimated from word count because M6 carries 38 spoken words (about 13 s) in a 5 s slot and M7 has no spoken line. So M6 runs 29 to 42, the effort dial gets a 1 s music-only breath at 42, and M8 to M11 shift later (M11 loses its unspoken slack). M12 and M13 land back on the screenplay clock; the CTA plus glimpse ends near 90.8 s. All times are estimates to be conformed to the recorded audio; visuals land on or just before the word.

References

Documentary paper-collage inserts: a torn page excerpt sliding in with a single highlighter sweep
A long thermal till receipt unrolling off a counter next to a tiny one

Risks

Timing: M6 has 38 spoken words (about 13 s) in a 5 s slot and M7 has no spoken line. I re-timed from 29 s on and gave the dial a 1 s music-only breath, so this cut lands near 90.8 s, about 1 s over the screenplay's estimate. To get under 90 s, drop 'Yet' first as the screenplay says, or draw the dial under 'And the release is curated'.
M11 is the densest stretch: lanes, the A+ sheet, the models.yaml recording and the D7 bars all sit inside about 6.5 s of speech. If it reads as rushed, the D7 inset is the one to move to the comment card.
[CLIPS NEEDED]: the M2 montage has no footage yet. Do not mock up posts or handles; without three real, credited clips the beat needs a different visual.
[DUMMY]: D7 still carries placeholder numbers and a placeholder winner. Keep the stamp visible or swap in real results before publishing; a clean-looking bar chart of dummy data would read as a real result.
S4 and S5 are named but not described in the screenplay I received, so KF14 only ghosts them as outlines; their contents need the pipeline source drawing.
Text load: each paper beat lists a cut, a MY READ tag and an interview line. I kept one hero line of six words or fewer per frame and pushed the rest to small grey footers; even so, KF08, KF14, KF16 and KF17 can clutter on a phone. The interview lines are the first to move to the comment card.
Tips card and Saturday tease are shortened on screen from the screenplay wording to stay readable (the 13-word tease cannot be read in 1 s). Full wording should go on the comment card and in the caption; this needs the creator's approval.
Paper cuts must be real page crops, never re-typed. They say 'Claude Mythos 5.2' while the voice says Fable until M9, which may confuse viewers at KF08 and KF11.
Plate frame: Opus (290 g) is within 5 g of Fable (295 g). Keep the Opus tag neutral so the frame does not imply Fable beat Opus; the claim is only against Haiku, and '34% lower' is portion error, not accuracy.
Effort wording: the p.209 footnote says the best score was at xhigh, while the tip says 'high, not max'. The tips dial must click to HIGH as written; a viewer who pauses may notice the difference.
Tone drift: M5 and M6 (failure mode, cheating) can slide into the trial reel's fear look. Keep coral to small marks, no red wash, no drone, a playful 'uh-oh'.
No logos or brand marks for the model maker or Instagram, no real-looking shop receipts; model names as plain text only.
The full pipeline is unreadable in the half panel, so KF18 must stay full-frame and be laid out vertically.
models.yaml recording: check for secrets and legibility at phone size; never rebuild it as a graphic.
Captions and on-screen text must not stack: when a hero line is on screen, keep the caption to one short line or drop it for that frame.
The screenplay text I received cuts off inside the trial-differences section. The MAIN beats are complete, so this board is unaffected, but the trial board needs the full text.
