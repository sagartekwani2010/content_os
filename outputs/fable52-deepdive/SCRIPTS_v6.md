# WHAT MAKES THIS VERSION MORE POWERFUL

- **The creator is the protagonist.** The real routing file and "It thinks; the cheaper ones check and do" are the core of both reels, and the screenshot card works for someone who never pays for Claude.
- **Both hooks are paid off.** Main answers "where is it worth it" in its last line. Trial answers the part the facts can answer (how it shipped, not why) by 17 s, and keeps his meaning separate from yours at the end.
- **Three overlapping loops hold the middle.** "Where is it worth it?", "Guess which", and "one job I never give it" each open before the previous one closes. Fermat is out of the spoken track.
- **One number per beat, in one unit.** Everything is a percentage except the bill and the plate, as locked. All superseded figures (32x, ₹36, riddles, Elo) are gone.
- **The claims are the size the data supports.** "Almost forty", Fable vs Haiku only for "same scores", the one statistically clear food percentage, and the spoken condition in the Trial hook leave little for a fact-checker to pull on.
