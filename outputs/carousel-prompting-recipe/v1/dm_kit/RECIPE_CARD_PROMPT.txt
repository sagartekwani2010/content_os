THE RECIPE-CARD PROMPT (copy, fill the blanks, paste into any AI chat)

Serves: [who it is for: the people, their goal, any must-haves]
Ingredients: [what you already have: facts, notes, files, what's in the kitchen]
Method: [the shape of the answer you want back: steps, bullets, a table, length, units]
Tastes like: [one example to aim at: "like X, but Y"]
Don't: [your limits: what to avoid, time, budget, tone]
Taste and adjust: [ask it to check its own work against your goal, and end with one question you could answer to make it better]


FILLED EXAMPLE (the exact prompt from my test, 22 Nov 2026)

Serves: 2 people, vegetarian, about 30 g protein each.
Ingredients (what is in my kitchen): 200 g paneer, moong dal, onion, tomato, spinach, curd, basic Indian spices, oil. No oven.
Method (what I want back): numbered steps with grams for every ingredient and minutes for every step, then the protein per serving.
Tastes like: dal tadka, but lighter.
Don't: no deep frying, no cream, nothing that takes more than 30 minutes.
Taste and adjust: check your protein maths against the 30 g goal and say if it falls short. End with one question I could answer to make it better.

What came back (both runs): a palak paneer moong dal in about 25-27 minutes, grams and minutes in the steps, no oven, and one question at the end. The AI put protein at about 34 g per serving. That is the AI's estimate, not verified nutrition: check labels or a nutrition database before you rely on it.
