**Lemon Chickpea Spinach Pasta** (20 minutes)

- 180 g pasta
- 1 can chickpeas, drained
- 3 handfuls spinach
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- ½ tsp chilli flakes
- Juice of 1 lemon
- Salt, pepper, grated parmesan (optional)

1. Boil pasta; reserve ½ cup of the water.
2. Heat oil, sizzle cumin and chilli for 30 seconds.
3. Add chickpeas, fry 5 minutes.
4. Stir in spinach until wilted.
5. Toss in pasta, lemon juice and a splash of pasta water. Season and serve.
