**Paneer Tomato Skillet with Rice (serves 2)**

2. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a pan; add 1 tsp cumin seeds and 1 tsp grated ginger.
3. Add 2 chopped tomatoes, ½ tsp turmeric, 1 tsp coriander powder, salt. Cook 6 minutes until soft.
