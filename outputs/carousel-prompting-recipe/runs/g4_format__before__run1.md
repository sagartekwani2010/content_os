# g4_format · before · run 1

## Step 1 prompt

How do I deadlift with correct form?

## Step 1 output

Here is the conventional barbell deadlift, step by step.

**Setup**
1. **Feet:** Stand hip-width apart with the bar over your midfoot, about 2-3 cm from your shins.
2. **Grip:** Hinge down and grip the bar just outside your legs, without moving the bar.
3. **Shins:** Bend your knees until your shins touch the bar. Your hips will end up above your knees and below your shoulders.
4. **Back:** Lift your chest to flatten your back. Keep your spine neutral, neither rounded nor over-arched, with your neck in line with it.
5. **Brace:** Take a big breath into your belly and tighten your core as if bracing for a punch.
6. **Lats:** Pull the slack out of the bar and think "bend the bar around your shins". Your arms stay straight.

**The lift**
1. **Push:** Push the floor away with your legs, as in a leg press, instead of yanking the bar with your back.
2. **Bar path:** Keep the bar in contact with your legs the whole way up, travelling in a straight vertical line.
3. **Timing:** Let your hips and shoulders rise at the same rate.
4. **Lockout:** Stand tall and squeeze your glutes. Don't lean back.

**Lowering**
1. **Hinge first:** Push your hips back, then bend your knees once the bar passes them.
2. **Control:** Keep the bar close and lower it under control.
3. **Reset:** Re-brace before each rep.

**Common mistakes**
- **Rounded lower back:** This is usually a sign of too much weight or a lost brace.
- **Bar drifting forward:** This puts far more load on your back.
- **Hips shooting up first:** This turns the lift into a stiff-leg deadlift.
- **Jerking the bar:** Build tension first, then lift.
- **Squatting it:** If your hips start too low, the bar has to go around your knees.

**Getting started**
Start light, with the empty bar or a bar raised on plates to mid-shin height. Film yourself from the side to check that your back is flat and the bar path is vertical. Add weight only while the form holds.

If you have a history of back problems, get a coach to watch a few sessions in person.
