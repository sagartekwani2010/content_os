DAY 1 BRIEF FOR YOUR AI TRAINER: 8 LINES (paste the ones you need under your ask)

1. Who you are:    This is for me: [age], [job], [what I eat], [my timings].
2. How you want it: Answer in 5 bullets, in the order I do them, max 8 words each.
3. Your limits:    Must: [your limits]. Must not: [what is off the table].
4. Ask first:      Before you start, ask me up to 3 questions.
5. No guessing:    Use only what I told you. Where you need something I didn't give, write [ASK ME] instead of assuming.
6. Examples:       I like ones like "[example 1]" and "[example 2]".
7. Check-in:       Give me the first part only. Then stop and ask me how it went.
8. Show the maths: Show the number next to each item, add them up, and tell me honestly if the total misses my target.

What each line changed in my test (Claude Fable 5.2, 22 Nov 2026, each ask run twice, test personas, not me):
1. A generic diet became one built around a canteen lunch and a 9:30 pm dinner.
2. A deadlift answer of over 300 words became 5 short cues.
3. A chicken and paneer bowl became a one-pot soya and moong dal khichdi (price/protein: the AI's estimates).
4. A full-gym plan became 3 questions first, then a dumbbell plan around a sore knee (scripted test reply).
5. "Start at about 2,000 kcal" (a guess) became "[ASK ME]" for height, age and activity.
6. Group names copied the tone of the examples (the smallest change of the 8).
7. A fixed 12-week table became 2 weeks, a check-in, and a changed week 3 after a sore shin (scripted test reply).
8. Per-item numbers whose totals add up; the lazy answer's stated total did not match its own meals.
